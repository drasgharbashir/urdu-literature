Why do gardeners plant gourds,
if the garden is not a beggar for wine? [TN: dried gourds were used as wine flasks.]

Though you are in every single thing,
there is nothing like you.

Yes, do not be deceived by existence:
however much they say it is, it is not.

Pass beyond joy, so that there is no grief:
if there were no spring month, there would be no winter month.

Why does the ascetic refuse the cup?
This is wine, not the vomit of the fly. [TN: honey.]

Ghalib, it is neither existence nor non-existence:
after all, what are you, you who "are not"?

Do not ask for the recipe of the ointment for the heart's wound,
for its chief ingredient is diamond dust.

After many days your neglect has produced
that one glance which is, to all appearances, less than a glance.

◆

The wine takes its bright colour from your lips:
the line of the cup is wholly the flower-picker's gaze.

May this frenzied head get some justice one day!
For a lifetime it has worshipped the longing for a pillow.

It is fair if the rose does not hear the wretched nightingale's laments,
for the rose's ear is stuffed with the cotton of dew.
