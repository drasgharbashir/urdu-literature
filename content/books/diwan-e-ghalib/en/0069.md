Longing has the habit of drawing out lament every moment;
the heart is in such a state that it panics at taking a breath.

May the evil eye stay far from your gathering of joy, bravo, bravo!
If my lament goes there, it turns into song.

Though the way of neglect veils the secret of love,
we are so lost that she finds us out.

Hearing of her gatherings, the grieving heart here
sinks like the image of the stranger's wish.

Becoming a lover, that fairy-faced one grew more delicate:
the more her colour fades, the more it shows.

How proud her portrait is even with its painter!
The more he draws it, the more it draws away.

Asad, my shadow flees from me like smoke:
who can stay near me, whose soul is on fire?

The bedding's form kept me crying out warmly;
then the cold of the nights gave me shelter in separation.

I know the truth of the cash and credit of both worlds:
my high ambition took me away from myself.

The multiplying of unity is the worship of illusion:
these idols of fancy have made me an infidel.

Even in imagining the longing for the rose there was no anxiety left:
what strange rest my winglessness gave me!
