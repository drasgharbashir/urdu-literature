Manifestation of God's grace, soul and heart of the *Seal of the Prophets*;
qibla of the Prophet's family, Kaaba of the creation of certainty.

Where that wealth of creation walks in pride,
every handful of dust there is a copy of the picture of the earth.

Wherever his footprint shows its radiance,
that handful of dust holds in trust the honour of both worlds.

From the relation to his name the earth has such rank
that the back of the sky stays bent for ever before the earth's pride.

It is his kindness to creation that is included, so that always
the breath of the morning breeze is perfumed with the scent of the rose.

The cut of his sword is talked of in the world:
may the thread of creation not be severed somewhere!

Such is his infidel-burning radiance that it breaks,
like a lover's colour, the splendour of the idol-house of China.

O refuge of souls! O giver of bounty to heart and soul! O King!
By the verdict of certainty you are the heir of the Seal of the Prophets.

For your pure body, the Prophet's shoulder was the pulpit;
for your noble name, the brow of the Throne is the seal-stone.

Who can praise you, except the Necessary Being?
Only if the candle's flame could adorn the candle.

On your threshold the grain of the stone's mirror
is the inscription of the service of *Gabriel*, the trusted one.

Ready to be scattered at your door are the offerings
God gave to men of dust: life, heart and faith.

For your praise are heart and soul, palate and tongue;
for your salutation are tablet and pen, hand and brow.
