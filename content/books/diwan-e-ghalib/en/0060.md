On the carpet of helplessness there was one heart, a single drop of blood, and that too
stays head down, ready to drip.

I stayed offended with that playful one a while, out of formality:
formality aside, that too was a kind of madness.

When can the thought of death comfort the hurt heart?
That too is a feeble prey in the net of my desire.

Would that I had not lamented! How was I to know, friend,
that that too would increase the pain within?

Do not pride yourself so on the cut of cruelty's sword:
that too is one wave of blood in my river of restlessness.

Why ask the cupbearer of the heavens for the wine of pleasure?
He too sits holding a few overturned cups.

Ghalib, in my heart is longing for union and complaint of separation:
may God bring the day when I tell her this, and that too.

In the gathering of idols, talk is with offended lips:
I am tired of such seekers of flattery.

The circling of the cup is the cause of the wine's distress:
put the wine-jar to my lips all at once.

Ascetic, the drinkers at the tavern door are insolent:
never take the side of these mannerless ones.

See faithfulness's cruelty, that in the end my life departed,
though it was so bound to my lips.
