Who would help madness if not nakedness?
The torn collar has a claim on my neck.

Like burnt paper, the magic of restlessness
ties a thousand mirrors of the heart to the wing of a single quiver.

How much we demand of the heavens for our past pleasures!
We think the stolen goods a debt the robber owes us.

I, and that enemy who knows grief without cause, who
blames the window's eye for looking, because of the sun's rays.

If you long for your true self, give yourself to annihilation:
the straw's good fortune depends on the furnace.

Asad, what kind of wounded man is he, who says to the killer:
"Practise your coquetry; the blood of both worlds is on my neck."

I bear the cruelty on purpose, since the fair ones love you:
without formality, in the end you will find a rival like yourself.

### Radif ze (ز)

Do not think me idle, for like the dawn and the sun
the scar of love still adorns the collar of my shroud.

The poor take pride in gold they have lost:
I still sell flowers, the bright colour of an old scar.

In the tavern of my heart there is not even dust here,
yet the idol skilled in cruelty still yawns for more.
