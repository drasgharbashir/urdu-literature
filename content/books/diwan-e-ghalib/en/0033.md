I die of grief that there is no one in the world
to mourn love and faithfulness after me.

Ghalib, I weep at love's helplessness:
whose house will the flood of calamity go to after me?

### Radif re (ر)

Never mind these walls and doors before my eyes:
for the gaze of longing, walls and doors are wings and feathers.

The flood of love so changed my dwelling
that my walls and doors became nothing but walls and doors.

It is not shadow: hearing news of the beloved's coming,
the walls and doors went a few steps ahead to meet her.

How cheap the wine of radiance has become,
that in your street every wall and door is drunk.

If you are mad with waiting, come,
for walls and doors are shops for the goods of the eye.

When did I ever gather such a storm of weeping
that the walls and doors should fall at my feet?

She is staying next door to me, and from her shadow
walls and doors are sacrificing themselves to walls and doors.

Without you the life of my house galls my eyes:
I always weep when I look at the walls and doors.

Do not ask about the ecstasy of the flood's arrival:
the walls and doors lie dancing from end to end.

Do not tell anyone that Ghalib has no confidant in this age:
only the walls and doors share the secret of love.
