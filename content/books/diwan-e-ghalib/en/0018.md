If the counsellor has imprisoned me, very well, let it be so;
but will these ways of love's madness leave me?

We are born servants of her tresses; why would we run from chains?
We are captives of faithfulness; why would prison frighten us?

Asad, there is now a famine of love's grief in this flourishing city;
granted, we will live in Delhi, but what will we eat?

How many pleasures of action desire has!
If there were no dying, what would be the taste of living?

What is the point of pretending not to know?
How long, O you who are all coquetry, what is this, what is that?

I see favours given where they do not belong;
why complain of colourful complaints?

I want a fearless glance;
what use are indifferences that test my composure?

The blaze of a burning straw lasts one breath:
what does desire care for the honour of faithfulness?

Breath is a wave in the ocean of self-forgetting:
why complain of the cupbearer's neglect?

I have no head for robes perfumed with scent:
why grieve over the wanderings of the morning breeze?

The heart of every drop is an instrument playing "I am the ocean":
we belong to it; who would ask about us?

Listen, you who plunder the goods of faithfulness, listen:
what sound does the breaking of the heart's price make?
