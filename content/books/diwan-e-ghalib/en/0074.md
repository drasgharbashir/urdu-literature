What news do you ask of those who suffer love's hardships?
Little by little those people became grief from head to foot.

How can your faithfulness make amends? For in the world
many cruelties besides yours were done to us.

We went on writing madness's tales that drip with blood,
though our hands were cut off for it.

God, the fierceness of your temper, for fear of which
the parts of lament in my heart became each other's food!

The victory of the lustful is to give up love's battle:
the feet that lifted in flight became their banners.

A few laments of ours were entrusted to us in non-existence:
those that could not be drawn out there came here and became breath.

Asad, even in begging we did not give up our amusement:
when we became beggars we fell in love with the generous.

◆

Have pity, tyrant, for what is the being of a snuffed lamp?
The pulse of one sick with faithfulness is the smoke of a snuffed lamp.

It is the longing for amusement that keeps us restless:
otherwise, here, lack of splendour is the profit of a snuffed lamp.

◆

The eye of the fair is musical even in silence:
you would say collyrium is the smoke of a flame's voice.

The lovers' frame is the instrument of an ill-tuned fortune:
lament is, as it were, the sound of the planets turning.
