Asad is dying: come, faithless one, for God's sake!
This is the place to drop the veil and bid composure farewell.

◆

Why should the idols' eye not be lost in neglect, why not?
That is, this invalid must abstain from looking.

Even as I die, the longing to see her will remain:
alas for failure, that that infidel's dagger is sharp!

Asad, seeing the rose's cheek, I remembered the beloved's face:
the surge of the spring season stirs up longing.

◆

I do not tolerate even my own envy:
I die, and yet I do not long for her.

Behind the veil she has a hidden bond with the stranger:
the veil of appearance is that she does not veil herself.

This is what makes the lustful despair:
you speak ill of Ghalib; you are not doing well.

◆

Seeing me secretly eager to shake off my hem,
my nakedness tied me to my body.

I became the whetstone of the beloved's glance-sword:
bravo for me! How blessed my heavy life is!

Why should she not be indifferent? Her mind is at ease:
she knows I am absorbed in asking after her in secret.
