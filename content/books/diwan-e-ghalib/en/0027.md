I am collyrium freely given to the eye; my price is this:
that the buyer's eye stays in my debt.

Give me leave to lament, lest, cruel one,
my hidden grief should show on your face.

◆

The careless one, fancying her charms, adorns herself; otherwise here
not even a lock of grass lacks the comb of the breeze.

Do not expect the pleasure of longing from the gathering of cups, for colour
is a bird escaped from the net in this hunting ground.

If mercy accepts it, it is not far-fetched:
not offering excuses for sin, out of shame.

With what joy I go to the killing field,
for the skirt of my gaze is filled with roses from the thought of wounds.

Asad, my life is spent longing for one warm glance:
the moth is the advocate for your plaintiff.

◆

Delicacy cannot show itself without some coarseness:
the garden is the verdigris on the mirror of the spring breeze.

The shore's self-restraint is no match for the river's surge:
where the cupbearer is, any claim of sobriety is void.

◆

The joy of the drop is to lose itself in the river;
pain, passing all limits, becomes its own cure.
