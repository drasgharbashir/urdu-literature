The grain of the sword is known to come from another spring:
I am that grass which poison-water makes grow.

The aim is absorbed in watching the heart break:
someone is taking me into the hall of mirrors.

Lament is the wealth of one whole world, and the world a handful of dust:
the sky looks to me like a turtle-dove's egg.

In life she used to make me get up from the gathering:
let me see who lifts me now that I am dead.

◆

If I become an echo, I am a burden on the mountain's mind:
without formality, O leaping spark, what is one to become?

Like an egg, this corner of the cage is a shame to wing and feather:
life would begin anew if one were set free.

◆

Drunkenness is destroyed by the taste for the cupbearer's heedlessness:
the wave of wine is one sleepy eyelash.

There is no desire in the heart but the wound of coquetry's sword:
even the pocket of fancy is torn by your hands.

Asad, from the surge of madness nothing can be seen:
the desert is a handful of dust in our eye.

◆

The movement of *Jesus's* lips rocks the cradle:
the sleep of one slain by the idols' ruby lips is heavy. [TN: Jesus's breath raised the dead.]
