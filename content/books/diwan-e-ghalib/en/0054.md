I am ashamed of love's plundering:
there is nothing in the house but the longing to build.

Asad, my verses are now only for amusement:
it is clear there is no profit at all in showing skill.

◆

Do not show me an unopened bud from afar, saying "like this":
I am asking for a kiss; tell me with your mouth, "like this."

Why ask about the ways of charm? Without a word
from her every gesture this grace emerges: "like this."

At night, having drunk wine, bringing the rival with her,
may God let her come here, but may God not let it be "like this."

When I asked, "What passed with the stranger last night?", look:
she came and sat before me, and gave a look, "like this."

Why not sit silent before her in the gathering?
Even in her silence the meaning is "like this."

I said, "The gathering of grace should be empty of strangers":
hearing it, the teasing tyrant got me up and out, "like this?"

When the beloved asked me, "How do the senses go?",
seeing my selflessness, the wind began to blow, "like this."

When did I remember how to stay in the beloved's street?
The bewilderment of the footprint held up a mirror: "like this."

If you think that longing fades in union,
the wave in the ocean of water thrashes hand and foot: "like this."

Whoever asks how *Rekhta* (Urdu verse) can be the envy of Persian,
read him Ghalib's poetry once and say, "like this."
