My heart trembles at the burden of the shining sun:
I am the dewdrop that sits on a desert thorn.

Even here the honoured Joseph did not give up adorning his house:
the whiteness of Jacob's eyes is plastered on the prison.

I have been taught the lesson of self-forgetting since the days
when Majnun wrote *lam-alif* (the first letters a schoolboy learns) on the school wall.

How free from worry about ointment I would be
if the pieces of my heart made peace with the salt-cellar!

In the realm of love there is no scroll of coquetry
whose address is not sealed with the back of the eye.

Seeing me now, I remember the cloud tinged with sunset,
for in your absence fire rained on the garden.

What could be left but the flight of the longing for coquetry?
The dust of the martyrs faces a furious wind of doom.

Ghalib, do not fight the counsellor; what if he was harsh?
After all, we too have our way with our collar.

◆

The clarity of the mirror's wonder becomes its rust in the end:
standing water changes colour at last.

The means of pleasure and rank brought no cure for madness:
even the emerald cup became a leopard's spot for me at last.
