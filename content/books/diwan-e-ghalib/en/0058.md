Ghalib, a single hope is leading me somewhere:
the path of the road for me is the pull of the *kaf* of *karam* (kindness). [TN: the hooked letter *kaf* begins *karam*.]

◆

Let us go now and live where there is no one:
no one to talk with, no one who shares our tongue.

One should build a house that has no walls and door:
no neighbour, and no watchman.

If one falls ill, let there be no one to nurse;
and if one dies, no one to mourn.

### Radif he (ہ)

Every wall and door of the house of grief is green as a meadow:
if this is its spring, do not ask about its autumn.

Helpless, bear even the longing for loneliness:
do not ask about the hardship of the road and the cruelty of companions.

◆

From the sun to the atom, it is heart upon heart, and the heart is a mirror:
the mirror faces the parrot from all six directions. [TN: parrots were taught to speak by placing a mirror before them.]

### Radif ye (ی)

A hundred radiances are before you if you raise your eyelashes:
where is the strength to bear the favour of seeing?

The allowance for the living of love's madness is drawn on stones:
that is, we must still bear the favour of children. [TN: children throw stones at the madman.]
