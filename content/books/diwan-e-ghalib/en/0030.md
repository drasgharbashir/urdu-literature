If anyone lives on safe until Doomsday,
still one day he must die, sir, safe and sound.

For my heart, love that drinks pure blood
writes, "May my lord and benefactor be safe."

In spite of the enemy I am a martyr of faithfulness:
congratulations, congratulations! Safe, safe!

If there are no means to grasp the meaning,
let the magic show of the outward form be safe.

Ghalib, my eyes closed even as I was opening them:
my friends brought her to my bedside, but at what hour!

If the beloved's market has cooled at the coming of down on her cheek,
perhaps that down was the smoke of a snuffed-out candle.

O heart that does not think of the end, restrain your longing:
who can bear the radiance of the beloved's face?

O wonder that ruins the house, look at the spectacle:
like a footprint, I am left behind by the beloved's walk.

In love I was killed by the cruelty of jealousy of the rival:
in the end I am slain by the enemy, though I was sick for the friend.

Our eyes are bright that the heart of that heartless one is glad:
our blood-filled eye is the beloved's brimming cup.
