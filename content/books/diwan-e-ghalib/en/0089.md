If kindness ever comes into her mind toward me,
remembering her cruelties she grows shy of me.

O God, the effect of the heart's attraction is reversed:
the more I draw her, the more she draws away from me.

She ill-tempered, and my tale of love so long!
In short, even the messenger panics at me.

There, that suspicion; here, this weakness:
she cannot ask, and I cannot speak.

O hopelessness, let me recover! What a calamity
that the hem of the beloved's thought is slipping from my hand!

Formality aside, grant that it is only gazing, but
let her be seen: when can I bear to see this cruelty?

In love's battle it was the feet that were wounded first:
I can neither flee nor stand my ground.

It is a calamity, Ghalib, that she should travel with the rival,
that infidel whom I cannot even entrust to God.

I am so thin that if you give me a place in the gathering,
I take the blame if anyone can see me and point me out.

What wonder if pity comes to her when she sees me?
If only someone by some trick could take me there.

Do not show your face, then, do not; but in the manner of anger
open the curtain and at least show me your eyes.

She is so pleased with my captivity that if I
became her tresses, she would tangle me in the comb.
