In the workshop of existence, the tulip is fitted out with a scar:
the lightning on the harvest of comfort is the farmer's warm blood.

The bud's provision of safety lasts only until it opens:
for all its composure, the rose's sleep is troubled.

How can we bear the pain of restlessness?
The scar is the back of helplessness's hand, the flame a straw held in the teeth. [TN: to hold a straw in the teeth is to beg for mercy.]

Ghalib, grass is growing from the walls and door:

At her simplicity, the longing to die is in my heart:
I am helpless, for the dagger is again in the killer's hand.

See the pleasure of speech, that whatever she said
I felt as if it too was in my heart.

Though with every kind of ill, yet for all that,
the mention of me in that gathering is better than I am.

Just the crowding of despair will bring it to dust,
this one pleasure that is in our fruitless effort.

Why suffer the pains of the road? Helplessness is in love:
the foot we have at the journey's end cannot be lifted.

Grant our heart is a flowerbed of the fire of hell:
in whose clay is the tumult of Doomsday?

Ghalib's frenzied heart is a talisman of twisting and turning:
have pity on your own desire, for in what difficulty it is!
