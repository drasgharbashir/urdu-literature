The intoxications are fresh with colour, the instruments drunk with joy:
the flask of wine is the green cypress by the stream of song.

Companion, do not say "Do not disturb the friend's gathering of pleasure":
there even my lament is taken for song.

◆

The display of the teeth's playful grace is for laughter:
the claim of the friends' togetherness is a thing to laugh at.

The pleasure of restlessness is forbidden to the trouble of dejection:
otherwise, pressing the teeth into the heart is the basis of laughter.

In non-existence the bud is absorbed in learning from the rose's end:
a whole world sits knee to forehead in thought behind laughter.

Friends deny the inner burning, or else here
the heart is an ocean of weeping and the lips acquainted with laughter.

◆

Careless beauty is the buyer of the goods of radiance:
the mirror is the knee of thought that invents radiance.

How long, O awareness, will you gamble away the colour of the spectacle?
If the eye is open, it is the embrace of radiance's farewell.

◆

Until someone produces the mouth of a wound,
it is hard for anyone to open a way to speak with you.
