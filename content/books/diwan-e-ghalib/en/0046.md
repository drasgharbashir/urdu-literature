Rough-and-tumble is not the way of that embodiment of grace:
Ghalib, it was we who made the first move one day.

◆

Be kind and call me whenever you wish:
I am not time gone by, that cannot come again.

In my weakness, why complain of the strangers' taunts?
A word is not a head, that I could not even lift it.

Tyrant, I simply cannot find poison, or else
is it an oath by meeting you, that I could not even take it?

◆

No scheme can stop my wandering in the desert:
there is a whirl in my feet, not a chain.

Longing drives me into that desert where
the path is nothing but the gaze of a painted eye.

The longing for the pleasure of torment remains:
the path of faithfulness is nothing but the sword's edge.

May the grief of eternal despair stay sweet to me:
I am glad if my lament is not degraded by seeking effect.

Where the wound on the head heals, the head itches:
the pleasure of the stone is beyond what words can say.

When kindness grants leave for boldness and insolence,
there is no fault but the shame of the fault.

Ghalib, this is my belief, in the words of *Nasikh* (a Lucknow poet, Ghalib's contemporary):
"He who does not believe in *Mir* (the great poet of Delhi) is himself without a share."
