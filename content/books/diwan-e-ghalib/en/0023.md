Suspicion did not want her to walk out eagerly:
it took each bead of sweat on her face for a wondering eye.

From my own weakness I knew she would be ill-tempered:
from the pulse of the straw I knew the heat of the burning flame.

On love's journey weakness sought rest:
at every step I took my own shadow for a bedchamber.

Until death my heart ran from the beloved's eyelashes:
it thought warding off the arrowhead of fate was so easy.

Asad, why did you give her your heart, thinking her faithful?
You erred in taking an infidel for a Muslim.

Again I remembered my tearful eyes:
my heart, my liver, thirsted to cry out.

Doomsday had not yet caught its breath
when I remembered again the hour of your departure.

The simplicity of longing: that is,
I remembered again that magic of the glance.

Excuse my weakness, O heart's longing!
I was lamenting, and I remembered my liver.

Life would have passed anyway;
why did I have to remember your street?

Alas, where is that courage to cry out?
Tired of my heart, I remembered my liver.

My thoughts go again to your lane;
it seems I have remembered my lost heart.
