After killing me he repented of his cruelty:
alas for the repentance of one so quick to repent.

Ghalib, pity the fate of that cloth four knots long
whose fate is to become a lover's collar.

◆

Last night my hangover of longing for the cupbearer was like the Day of Resurrection:
to the very edge of the ocean of wine, everything looked like a house of yawning.

One step of madness opened the lesson of the book of possibility:
the path was the binding that held together the pages of both worlds' desert.

Who can stop Laila's wild wanderings?
The house of desert-roaming *Majnun* had no door.

Do not ask how beauty's show of indifference was exposed:
her hand was pledged to henna, her cheek pawned to rouge.

The heart's lament scattered the leaves of my heart's pieces to the wind;
the memorial of that lament is an unbound *divan* (collection of poems).

◆

What effort will friends make in consoling me?
Will the nails not grow back by the time the wound heals?

If the honoured counsellor comes, my eyes and heart will be spread on his path;
but someone explain to me what he will explain.

Your indifference has gone beyond all limits, kind master; how long
shall we tell the state of our heart, and you only say, "What?"

Today I go there with sword and shroud tied on;
what excuse can he now make for not killing me?
