The wall is bent under the burden of the labourer's favour:
O you whose house is ruined, do not accept favours.

Either do not disgrace my wound of envy,
or lift the veil from your hidden smile.

◆

There should be a tavern in the shadow of the mosque:
near the eyebrow, the eye should be the *qibla* (direction of prayer) of needs.

You too have fallen in love with someone else:
after all, cruelty should have some retribution.

O heavens, do justice to the longing-worshipping heart:
yes, there should be some recompense for what is lost.

For the moon-faced ones I have learned to paint:
there should be some pretext for meeting.

Who, black-faced wretch, wants wine for pleasure?
I want a kind of self-forgetting day and night.

Ghalib, the branches grow from the root:
the word that is needed comes out of silence itself.

The colours of the tulip, the rose and the eglantine are different:
in every colour spring should be affirmed.

In the hour of self-forgetting the head should be at the foot of the wine-jar;
at the time of prayer the face should be toward the qibla.

That is, according to the turning of the cup of attributes,
the knower should always be drunk on the wine of the Essence.
