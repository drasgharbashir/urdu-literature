What effort counts in our own disgrace?
It must be the beloved who raises the tumult.

One whose hope depends on dying,
his hopelessness one should see.

Asad wants the fair ones:
one should look at your own face, sir!

Heedless one, for these moon-faced ones
the one who loves should also be good.

◆

At every step the distance of the goal is plain because of me:
the desert runs from my pace.

The lesson of the title of the spectacle is better with neglect:
the glance is the binding thread of the eyelashes because of me.

In the night of loneliness, from the wildness of the heart's fire,
my shadow kept fleeing from me like smoke.

The grief of lovers should not teach the idols simplicity:
how ruined the house of the mirror is because of me!

From the effect of the blisters, the path of madness's desert
is lit with lamps like a string of pearls because of me.

If self-forgetting were the bed that prepares for rest!
My bedchamber is full of me, like a shadow.

If you behead me in the longing to see you,
my glance would scatter like a candle's flower because of me.

The wildness of the loneliness of the night of separation, alas, alas!
My shadow hides from me in the sun of Doomsday.
