Look at me, if you have an eye that takes warning!
Listen to me, if you have an ear that heeds advice!

The cupbearer, with her radiance, is the enemy of faith and knowledge;
the singer, with her song, is the robber of composure and sense.

Either you saw at night that every corner of the carpet
was a gardener's skirt and a flower-seller's palm,

the delight of the cupbearer's walk and the taste of the harp's sound,
this a paradise for the eye, that a heaven for the ear,

or, if you come and look in the gathering at dawn,
there is neither that delight and burning, nor that fervour and uproar.

Burnt by the scar of parting from the night's company,
one candle is left, and that too is out.

These themes come into my thought from the Unseen:
Ghalib, the scratching of the pen is the voice of *Sarosh* (the angel of inspiration).

◆

Come, for my soul has no rest:
I have no strength for the cruelty of waiting.

They give paradise in exchange for life in this world:
the intoxication is not equal to the hangover.

Weeping drives me from your gathering:
alas, that I have no control over weeping!

It is vain to suspect me of a grudge:
there is no dust in the lovers' dust.

Draw from the heart the delight of the radiances of meaning:
there is no mirror of spring but the rose.
