The coming of the flood is the storm of the water's sound:
the footprint puts its finger in its ear, with the path.

Whose drunken eye has made the wine gathering a house of wildness?
A fairy's pulse is hidden in the flask by the wave of wine.

◆

Where the breeze combs the beloved's tresses,
the musk-pod is the mind of the deer of the desert of *Tatar* (the steppes famed for musk).

O God, whose radiance is bewilderment tracking?
The mirror is the carpet of the six directions of waiting.

Every atom is the dust of longing from want of room:
if this is the net, the expanse of the desert is the prey.

The heart is the plaintiff and the eye the defendant:
the case of gazing is being heard again.

The dew sprinkles water on the mirror of the rose petal:
O nightingale, it is the time of spring's farewell.

I have got stuck on the beloved's promise:
whether she comes or not, here there is waiting.

Do not pass unveiled toward Majnun's valley:
in every atom's veil there is a restless heart.

O nightingale, a handful of straw for the nest!
The storm of the coming of the spring season is near.

Do not lose your heart: if not news, then at least the walk;
O mindless one, the mirror holds the image.
