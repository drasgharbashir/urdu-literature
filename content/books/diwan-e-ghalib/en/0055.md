### Radif vav (و)

If your heart is chilled by envy, grow warm with watching:
perhaps the narrow eye will open from seeing much.

The taste for sin should match the heart's longing:
I would fill only one corner of my hem with all the water of the seven seas.

If that cypress-tall one should come, warm with her graceful walk,
every handful of the garden's dust would lament, like a turtle-dove.

We are past caring whether it be love:
treat us with something, even if it be enmity.

Weakness left in me no colour of companionship:
it is a burden on the heart, even the image of love.

I complain that you speak to me of the stranger,
even if only by way of complaint.

They say there is a cure born for every pain:
if so, why not a cure for the grief of love?

Loneliness has left me no dealings with anyone:
I bear even shame only from myself.

Man in himself is a Doomsday crowd of thoughts:
we count it company, even if it be solitude.

Shame is the uproar of failing courage:
take nothing from the world, not even a lesson.

Does the grief of losing life's leisure ever fade,
even if precious life is spent in worship?
