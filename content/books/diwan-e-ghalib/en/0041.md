A sigh needs a lifetime to take effect:
who lives until your tresses are won?

In the net of every wave is a ring of a hundred crocodile jaws:
let us see what befalls the drop before it becomes a pearl.

Love asks for patience, and desire is restless:
what colour should I give my heart until my liver turns to blood?

I grant that you will not neglect me, but
I will have turned to dust by the time you hear of it.

The sun's ray teaches the dew the lesson of annihilation:
I too exist only until a glance of kindness falls on me.

Heedless one, the leisure of life is no more than one glance:
the warmth of the gathering lasts as long as a spark's dance.

Asad, who can cure the grief of existence but death?
The candle burns in every colour until dawn.

### Radif gaf (گ)

If you are sure the prayer will be answered, do not pray:
that is, ask for nothing but a heart free of wanting.

The count of the scars of my heart's longing comes back to me:
O God, do not ask me to account for my sins.

### Radif lam (ل)

How utterly destroyed is the rose by the deceit of faithfulness!
The rose's laughter mocks the nightingale's trade.
