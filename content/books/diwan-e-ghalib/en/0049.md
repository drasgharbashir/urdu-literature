Be cruel, then, cruel! If kindness costs you too much,
you have no excuse of any colour for neglect.

We are plainly drinkers of the dregs of Jamshid's cup:
alas for the wine that is not pressed from grapes!

Ghalib, beside *Zuhuri* (the Persian poet) I am obscure:
the proof of my claim is that I am not famous.

◆

O inventor of cruelty, lament is nothing but a fine way of asking:
it demands cruelty; it is not a complaint of injustice.

Love, and labouring for Khusrau's pleasure-house, how fine!
We do not accept *Farhad's* (the stonecutter lover of Shirin) good name.

It is no less ruined either, but its breadth is known:
in the desert I have such comfort that I do not remember home.

For people of insight the storm of events is a school:
the slap of the wave is no less than the master's blow.

Alas for the oppression of submission, and alas for faithfulness!
She knows we have no strength to cry out.

Why is the dignity of the rose and tulip scattered in colour?
Unless they are lamps along the wind's path.

The flower-picker shuts it up under a basket of roses:
good news, O bird, there is no hunter in the garden!

It is as if her freshness proves itself through denial:
at her creation she was given a mouth that says "No."

Paradise is no less radiant than your street:
it is the same pattern, only not so crowded.
