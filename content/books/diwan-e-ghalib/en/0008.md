## Ghazals · radif alif (ا)

*Ghazal*: a lyric poem of rhyming couplets. In a *divan* the ghazals are arranged in alphabetical order by the last letter of their rhyme, and each section is headed *radif* with that letter.

Whose playful pen does the picture lodge its complaint against?
Every figure in the portrait wears a paper robe.

Do not ask how hard it is to endure the pain of loneliness:
to turn evening into morning is like digging a channel for a river of milk.

Behold the power of uncontrollable longing:
the sword's very breath has broken out of the sword's breast.

Let understanding spread the net of listening as wide as it likes:
the meaning of my speech is the *anqa* (a mythical bird that no one has ever caught).

Ghalib, even in captivity my feet are on fire:
each link of my chain is a hair that has passed through flame.

◆

Wounds for a present, diamond dust for a gift, a scarred heart for an offering:
congratulations, Asad, the comforter of aching souls has come.

◆

No one but *Qais* (Majnun, the lover of Laila who fled to the desert) ever came forward to act;
yet the desert itself was as narrow as the envious eye.
