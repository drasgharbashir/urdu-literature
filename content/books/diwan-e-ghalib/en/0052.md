Never mind room for the strangers' enmity:
from weakness there is not even desire for the beloved in my heart.

Fear my piteous laments, for God's sake:
after all, they are not just the song of a caged bird.

My heart contends with the beloved's ranks of eyelashes,
though it lacks even the strength to bear a thorn's prick.

O God, why should one not die for this simplicity?
They fight, and there is not even a sword in their hand.

I have often seen Asad in private and in company:
if he is not mad, he is not sane either.

◆

In separation we look at the walls and the door,
now at the morning breeze, now at the messenger.

That she should come into my house is God's power:
now I look at her, now at my house.

Let no evil eye fall on her hand and arm:
why do these people look at the wound of my liver?

Why should I look at the jewels on the edge of your cap?
I look at the high fortune of the ruby and the pearl.

◆

There is no wound in my body worth stitching:
the thread of despair's tear has become the string in the needle's eye.

The ruin of my house has stopped the taste for spectacle:
the flood's foam remains in the chink like cotton.
