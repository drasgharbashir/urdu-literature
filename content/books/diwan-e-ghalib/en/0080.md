I am a friend of neglect; my helplessness has a lofty mind:
if you avoid me, my place too is empty.

The world stayed inhabited because the men of courage were absent:
however full the cups and jars, the tavern is empty.

◆

When does she listen to my story?
And then, told in my own words!

Do not ask about the pricking of the bloodthirsty glance:
look at my raining of blood-water.

What will my friends tell, weeping over me,
except my distracted way of telling?

I am lost to myself in the desert of fancy:
forgetting is my mark.

My rival in the contest stands opposed:
he stopped short, seeing my flow.

I keep the worth of a stone on the road:
my dearness is very cheap.

I am the whirlwind on the road of restlessness:
the storm of longing is my founder.

When her mouth could not be found,
my knowing nothing was revealed. [TN: the beloved's mouth is proverbially too small to see.]

Ghalib, weakness has made me helpless:
my youth is the shame of old age.
