Though life moves with lightning's pace,
let there at least be leisure to turn the heart to blood.

O unjust heavens, give something at least:
let it be only leave to sigh and cry out.

We too will learn the habit of submission:
let indifference be your habit.

Are we abandoning faithfulness?
If not love, let it be calamity.

Asad, let the teasing of the beloved go on:
if not union, let it be longing.

In my rest the reproach is fair to me:
the morning of home is to me a laugh that shows the teeth.

My soul seeks that fire-breathed singer
whose voice would be for me the flash of annihilation's lightning.

Drunkenly I travel the road of the valley of fancy,
so that I have no aim of returning.

Since you go so unveiled in the garden,
I have begun to feel shy before the scent of the rose.

Why would the affair of my heart be revealed to anyone?
The choice of verses disgraced me.
