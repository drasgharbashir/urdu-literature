What a marvel, hearing that Laila had come to Qais's desert,
he said in amazement, "Does this happen in the world too?"

Ghalib, I pity her delicate heart:
do not make that infidel eager to test love.

◆

Having given her heart, she too took to sitting alone:
at last we got justice for our loneliness here.

All the parts of creation are ready to decline:
the sun in the sky is a lamp on the wind's path here.

◆

The stranger's sweet talk has worked:
she does not suspect love in us who have no tongue.

◆

We tie the morning breeze to your horse:
we too build castles in the air of theme. [TN: a pun on *hava bandhna*, "to boast" and literally "to tie the wind".]

Who has seen a sigh take effect?
We too are only boasting our own air.

Against your leisure, O life,
we tie lightning's feet with *henna* (the red dye that keeps a bride indoors till it dries).
