Ghalib, do not make your letter so long; write briefly,
for I am one who longs to tell the cruelties of separation.

◆

If the grief of the night of parting is not spoken,
the scar on the moon will plainly become a seal upon my mouth.

If the evening of separation turns one's courage to water like this,
the moonlight will become a flood that sweeps away the house.

I could kiss her foot while she sleeps, but
such things would make that faithless one suspicious.

We thought the heart would be spent on faithfulness; how could we know
it would be sacrificed to the test before that?

You have a place in everyone's heart; if you are pleased with me,
it will be as if the whole world is kind to me.

If the warm glance keeps teaching restraint,
the flame will hide in the straw as blood hides in the vein.

Do not take me to the garden, or else, at my state,
every fresh rose will become an eye shedding blood.

Alas if there is no judgement between you and me on Judgement Day!
Until now my hope is that it will be done there.

What good is it? Think, Asad, you too are wise after all:
it is friendship with a fool; you will only lose your life.

◆

Longing complains of lack of space even within the heart:
the ocean's restlessness was absorbed into the pearl.
