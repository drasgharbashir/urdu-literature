The world from end to end is the dust of Majnun's wildness:
how long can anyone think of Laila's lock?

Dejection is not the joy that favour composes:
yes, someone might take a place in the heart by becoming pain.

O friend, do not blame me for weeping:
after all, someone must untie the heart's knot some day.

When the liver's tear did not open a way to be asked after,
what use for anyone to disgrace his collar?

With pieces of the liver every thorn's vein is a rose branch:
how long must one be gardener to the desert?

The glance's failure is lightning that burns the sight:
you are not one whom anyone could gaze upon.

Every stone and brick is an oyster of the pearl of breaking:
it is no loss if anyone trades with madness.

Life did not suffice for the promise that tests patience:
where is the leisure for anyone to long for you?

The wildness of creation's nature breeds despair:
this pain is not one that anyone could fail to produce.

The idleness of madness has the pastime of beating its head:
when the hands are broken, what can anyone do then?

Asad, the beauty of the glow of the candle of speech is far:
first one must produce a melted heart.
