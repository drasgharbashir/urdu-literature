The turtle-dove a handful of ash, the nightingale a cage of colour:
O lament, what is the sign of a burnt liver?

Your temper has chilled the wildness of my heart:
being beloved and lacking courage is a strange calamity.

Helplessness, and the claim of being captive to love!
The pledge of faithfulness is a hand caught under a stone.

The state of the martyrs of the past became known:
the sword of cruelty is a mirror that shows their picture.

O ray of the world-illuminating sun, here too!
Like a shadow, a strange time has fallen on us.

Let me at least get credit for the longing for sins I did not commit,
O Lord, if there is punishment for these sins I did commit.

Ghalib, do not lose heart at people's indifference:
if you have no one, my dear, there is God.

◆

It is long since I had the beloved as my guest,
lighting up the gathering with the surge of the cup.

Again I am gathering my liver, torn to pieces:
it is long since I invited my eyelashes.

Again my breath is being stifled by careful conduct:
it is years since I tore my collar.

Again my breath is hot with spark-raining laments:
it is long since I went to see the lamps.

Again love has set out to ask after the heart's wound,
carrying a hundred thousand salt-cellars.
