The image of the coquettish idol's grace in the rival's embrace
would ask for a peacock's foot to serve as *Mani's* (the legendary painter's) pen.

You are so ill-tempered that you take bewilderment for a show;
grief is such a tale that it asks to be told distractedly.

That fever of love is a desire which, again like the candle,
asks the flame to send roots down to the pulse of the liver.

◆

The garden so delighted in your company
that every bud's opening into a flower is an opening of arms.

There, the battlement of indifference is ever on the heights;
here, lament still claims, the other way round, that it reaches.

Since grief teaches the ways of restraint,
every scar that appears is an eye showing itself.

◆

The wound that can be treated by stitching,
O Lord, write it into the fate of the enemy.

The thought of the henna-dyed fingertip is good:
at least a drop of blood appears in the heart.

Why fear the lovers' lack of courage?
Here no one listens to anyone's cry.

A hundred pities for that unlucky one who, Ghalib, all his life
longed for a quarrelsome idol,

whose liver no dagger ever touched with its mouth,
whose throat no blade ever asked after.
