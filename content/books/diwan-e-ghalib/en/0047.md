Love is not hopeless of effect:
giving up one's life is not the willow tree. [TN: the willow is proverbially fruitless.]

The kingdom has come from hand to hand:
the wine cup is not the ring of *Jamshid* (legendary Persian king, owner of a world-revealing cup).

Your radiance is the stuff of existence:
no atom is without the sun's reflection.

Let the secret of the beloved not be disgraced,
or else there is no mystery in dying.

I fear the turning of the colour of delight,
not the grief of eternal deprivation.

They say that people live on hope:
we have no hope even of living.

◆

It is the monsoon of the lover's tears; one should see
how the garden wall has burst open in a hundred places like a rose.

The claim to freedom is false with love of the rose:
the cypress, though free, is a captive of the garden.

◆

Do not take these glances in the pupil of the eye for glances:
they are sighs gathered in the black core of the eye's heart.
