Cupbearer, even the hangover of thirst is measured to the cup:
if you are a river of wine, I am the shore's yawning mouth.

◆

It is you who are not privy to the songs of the secret;
otherwise every veil here is the curtain of a musical instrument.

A faded colour is the spring dawn of beholding:
this is the hour for the flowers of coquetry to bloom.

You, and your swift glances towards others!
Me, and my pain from your long eyelashes!

I hold back my sighs only to spare myself; otherwise
I am food for a single soul-melting breath.

The flasks are leaping so with the ferment of wine
that every corner of the mat is the head of a bottle-juggler.

The heart demands more probing, for still
the fingernail owes a debt to this half-opened knot.

Asad, my breast, which was a treasury of the pearls of secrets,
has been plundered by the probing grief of separation.

◆

Last night, when the lightning of my heart's burning turned the clouds' courage to water,
every ring of the whirlpool was a whirling flame.

There, the excuse of rain held back her steps from coming;
here, from weeping, my pillow's cotton was the foam of a flood.

There, in adorning herself, she thought of stringing pearls;
here, in the crowd of tears, the thread of my gaze could not be found.
