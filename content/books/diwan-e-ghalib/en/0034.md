Why did I not burn at the brilliance of the beloved's face?
I burn to see my own strength to look.

People call me a fire-worshipper
when they see me so busy with sparking laments.

What honour is left for love where cruelty is common?
I hold back when I see you torment without cause.

She comes to kill me, but from the heat of jealousy
I die to see the sword in her hand.

The people's blood is proven on the neck of the flask:
the wave of wine trembles to see the way you walk.

Alas, the beloved withdrew her hand from cruelty
when she saw me greedy for the pleasure of torment.

We sell ourselves along with our goods of verse,
but only after testing the buyer's taste.

Tie the *zunnar* (the sacred thread of the infidel), break the hundred-bead rosary:
the traveller takes the road when he sees it level.

I had grown tired of these blisters on my feet;
I was glad to see the road full of thorns.

How suspicious she is of me: in my mirror
she sees the verdigris and takes it for a parrot's image.

The lightning of revelation should have fallen on me, not on Mount Sinai:
wine is given according to the drinker's capacity.

That head-breaking of distraught Ghalib!
I remembered it when I saw your wall.
