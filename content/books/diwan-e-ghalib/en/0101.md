### Selection from a qasida in praise of Ali al-Murtaza, peace be upon him

The world is nothing but the radiance of the beloved's oneness:
where would we be if beauty were not self-regarding?

The dispiritedness of the spectacle, with neither warning nor taste!
The loneliness of desire, with neither this world nor faith!

The song of the high and low of being and non-being is idle;
the mirror of the difference between madness and composure is vain.

The image of meaning is wholly the yawn of displaying form;
the word of truth is wholly the cup of the taste for praise.

The boast of knowledge is false, and the profit of worship well known:
this world and faith are the dregs of one cup of heedlessness.

Like the theme of faithfulness, wind in the hand of submission;
like the footprint, dust on the head of composure.

Love is the unravelling of the binding of the parts of the senses;
union is the rust on the face of the mirror of the beauty of certainty.

The mountain-digger is the hungry labourer of the rival's pleasure-house;
*Bisitun* (the mountain Farhad carved) is the mirror of Shirin's heavy sleep.

Who has seen the breath of the faithful raise fire?
Who has found an effect in the lament of sorrowing hearts?

I listen to the songs of the people of the world, but
I have neither the means for praise nor the mind for curses.

How idly I talk, that, God protect me,
I am wholly outside the manners of dignity and composure!

Write the sign of "*La hawl*" (there is no power but God's), O pen that writes ravings!
Say "*Ya Ali*", O nature close to temptation!
