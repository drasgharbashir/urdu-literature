Alas, God forbid, she and enmity?
O ashamed longing, what are you thinking?

Know that the Kaaba's robe is musk-scented from Ali's footsteps:
it is the navel of the earth, not the navel of the musk-deer.

The expanse of the horizons was too narrow for my wildness:
the sea is the sweat of the earth's shame.

Asad, do not be deceived by existence:
the whole world is a ring in the net of fancy.

◆

Do not dig and dig to ask what I complain of:
beware of my heart, for there is fire buried in it.

O heart, even this pain and grief are a blessing, for in the end
there is neither weeping at dawn nor sighing at midnight.

◆

In one place I had written the word "faithfulness", and that too was erased:
clearly the paper of your letter does not take ink.

Why should the soul not burn at the incompleteness of the taste for annihilation?
We do not burn, though our breath rains fire.

When fire is quenched in water a sound rises:
everyone in distress is forced to lament.

He alone pleads the excuse for every atom's drunkenness
whose radiance fills earth and sky to the brim.
