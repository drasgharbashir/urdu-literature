If I ever found leisure from the world's grief to raise my head,
looking at the sky became an occasion to remember you.

O Lord, how will the meaning of my letter be revealed?
That infidel has sworn to burn the paper.

It is easy for the flame of fire to wrap itself in silk,
but the art of hiding grief's burning in the heart is hard.

She wanted to come and see her wounded ones:
she rose to walk among the roses; see the playfulness of the pretext!

It was our simplicity to die at the favour of her grace:
tyrant, your coming was only a prelude to going.

It cannot bear the trampling of events,
my strength that was surety for bearing the idols' coquetry.

Ghalib, what can I say of the fine manners of the sons of the age?
He did me wrong, to whom I had done good again and again.
