Ghalib, weeping raised a tumult in my heart again:
the drop that had not come out as a tear came out a flood.

◆

He died of a threat who was not made for battle;
combative love wanted a real man.

Even in life the fear of death was with me:
my colour was pale before it ever flew.

I was compiling volumes of faithfulness;
the collection of my thoughts was still loose leaves.

The heart, as far as the liver, is now the shore of a river of blood;
on this road there was once a show of roses, and dust before that.

Does love's struggle with sorrow ever end?
Even if the heart was lost, the heart's pain remained.

My friends could not cure my madness:
even in prison my thoughts roamed the wilderness.

This unshrouded body belongs to wretched Asad;
may God forgive him, he was a rare free spirit.

◆

The counting of rosary beads pleased the idol who loves the hard:
the spectacle of carrying off a hundred hearts in one palm pleased him.

Thanks to having no heart, lasting despair is easy;
ease liked the hard knot we had tied.

The urge to stroll among roses mirrors the killer's indifference:
he liked the way the wounded one rolled in blood.
