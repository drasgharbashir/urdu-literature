In it, Asad, lakhs of desires are imprisoned for ever:
we know the blood-filled breast to be a prison house.

◆

Gather the heart's attachment through lament:
it is known that the chain's household goods are nothing but sound.

◆

He killed me in a strange land, far from home:
my God kept the honour of my loneliness.

Those rings of tresses lie in ambush, O God:
keep the honour of my claim to be free.

### Radif nun (ن)

I would borrow one sweet dream from my sleeping fortune, but
Ghalib, I fear: from where would I repay it?

◆

Where is that separation, and where that union?
Where are those nights and days, months and years?

Who has leisure for the business of longing?
Where is the taste for gazing at beauty?

Leave the heart: even that mind is gone.
Where is the tumult of passion for down and mole?

It came from the thought of one person:
where now is that grace of imagination?
