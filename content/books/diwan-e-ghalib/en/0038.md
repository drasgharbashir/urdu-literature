May that day come when from that tyrant
I suffer coquetry, instead of longing for it.

There is no drop of blood in my heart
that has not turned my eyelashes into flower-sprays.

O you whose glance stirs up everything,
O you whose cruelty is pure style!

You have appeared in glory: congratulations
on the shower of prostrations from the brow of devotion.

If you asked after me, no calamity followed:
I am poor, and you are kind to the poor.

Asadullah Khan is finished:
alas for that libertine lover of beauty!

### Radif sin (س)

Good news, O taste for captivity! There appears
an empty snare beside the captive bird's cage.

The liver thirsty for torment was not satisfied:
we let a stream of blood flow at the root of every thorn.

Alas, alas, my eyes closed even as they were opening:
what a time you came to this sick lover!

I too would not die by fits and starts if, instead of a tongue,
my comforter had a sharp dagger.

Go and sit in the lion's mouth, but O heart,
do not stand near the fair ones who wound hearts.

Seeing you, the garden so grows
that the rose reaches the corner of the turban of its own accord.
