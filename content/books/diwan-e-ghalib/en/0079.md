The breath of Qais is the eye and lamp of the desert:
if it is not the candle of Laila's black tent, so be it.

The splendour of a house depends on some uproar:
let it be a dirge of grief, if not a song of joy.

No longing for praise, no care for reward:
if there is no meaning in my verses, so be it.

Count the pleasure of the company of the fair a blessing:
Ghalib, if you did not live a natural span, so be it.

With what strange delight we walk ahead of the executioner,
my head two steps ahead of my feet in my own shadow!

Fate had meant me to be ruined by the wine of love:
it wrote only "ruined", and the pen could go no further.

The grief of the age shook off the intoxication of love's delight,
or else we too once enjoyed the pleasure of grief.

For God's sake give credit to this madness of longing,
that we reach her door before the messenger.

All the distress we have suffered all our life,
O curling locks, may it come before you.

The one wave of blood that flutters in heart and liver,
we in our conceit took it for breath.

Ghalib, she swears now to come to my funeral,
she who always used to swear by my life.
