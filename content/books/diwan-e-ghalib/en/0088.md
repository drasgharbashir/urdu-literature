When the fate of my house of grief began to be written,
it wrote me down among the causes of its ruin.

That infidel grows suspicious; would that I did not have
so great a taste for the song of the garden bird!

Alas, even there the uproar of Doomsday did not let me breathe:
the taste for ease of body had taken me to the grave.

Keep your promise to come: what kind of way is this?
Why have you made me the doorkeeper of my own house?

Yes, the joy of the coming of the spring season, bravo, bravo!
Again the passion for reciting ghazals is fresh in me.

God has given my brother life anew:
Ghalib, *Mirza Yusuf* (Ghalib's brother) is a second Joseph to me.

◆

Even in joy I remember the uproar of "O Lord!":
the smile under my lips has become the ascetic's rosary.

The opening of the bound heart is pledged to speech:
the schoolroom was to me a talisman of the alphabet lock.

O Lord, from whom should I seek justice for this distraction?
I now envy the prisoners their ease.

My nature longs for the pleasures of longing; what can I do?
From desire, my aim is the breaking of desire.

Ghalib, having given your heart, you too have become like me:
Mirza Sahib, you used to stop me from love!
