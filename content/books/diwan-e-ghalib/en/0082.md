Like quicksilver behind the mirror, warming it, we
are bewildered by the restless heart.

The rose's arms are opened for farewell:
come, O nightingale, the days of spring are going.

◆

In the world of composure and restraint, union is separation:
there must be a playful beloved and a mad lover.

From those lips a kiss will be had some day, yes!
What is needed is vain longing and a drinker's boldness.

◆

One should love the good as much as they deserve:
if they should love us, what more is needed?

It is a duty to beware the company of drinkers:
in place of wine, one should draw oneself away.

What did the heart think your loving was?
At last one should understand this too.

Do not tear your collar without the season of roses:
there should be some hint from that side too.

Strangeness is the veil of friendship:
you should stop hiding your face from us.

My enmity has lost the stranger:
how much of an enemy she is, one should see.
