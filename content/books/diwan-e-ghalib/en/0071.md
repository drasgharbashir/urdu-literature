Your glance went down from the heart to the liver:
it pleased both with a single gesture.

The breast is split open: how sweet the pleasure of release!
The trouble of veiling the liver's wound is gone.

Where are the intoxications of the night's wine?
Rise now, for the pleasure of the dawn sleep is gone.

My dust flies about in the beloved's street:
O wind, at last the longing for wings and feathers is gone.

Look at the charm of the footprint's style:
what flowers the wave of the beloved's walk has cut!

Every lustful one has made beauty-worship his way:
now the honour of the way of the discerning is gone.

There, even gazing did the work of a veil:
in drunkenness every glance scattered over your face.

The difference between tomorrow and yesterday was erased at once:
yesterday you left, and Doomsday passed over us.

Asadullah Khan, the age has killed you:
where are those passions, where has that youth gone?

Once again this heart is restless:
the breast is seeking a deadly wound.

Again the fingernail has begun to dig the liver:
the season of tulip-work has come.
