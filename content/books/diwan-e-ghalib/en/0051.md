Release from the prison of being? Who knows!
We describe the tear as headless and footless.

The rose opens from the intoxication of colour:
when do the drunk tie the fastenings of their robe?

Do not ask about the errors of themes:
people describe the lament as reaching its goal.

The helplessness of the prudent!
They put henna even on blisters.

Ghalib, the fair ones are simple and cunning:
they make pledges of faithfulness with us.

By Asad's life, the age is very sparing of torment:
otherwise we expect much more.

◆

In madness there is not even a *zunnar* on my shoulder:
that is, not even one thread is left in my collar.

I had offered my heart to the longing to see her;
when I looked, I had no strength even to see.

If meeting you is not easy, then it is easy:
the hard thing is that it is not even hard.

Life cannot pass without love, and here
I lack strength equal even to the pleasure of torment.

Through frenzy my head is a burden on my shoulders:
O God, in the desert there is not even a wall!
