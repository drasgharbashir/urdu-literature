Grief has erased even trust in existence:
to whom can I say that the scar is the mark of the liver?

At least there is so much trust in her faithfulness
that, Ghalib, we are happy that she is unkind.

◆

You are restless at my pain, alas, alas!
Tyrant, what became of your habit of neglect, alas, alas?

If your heart had not the courage for grief's tumult,
why then did you comfort me, alas, alas?

Why did you think of sharing my grief?
My friendship was your own enemy, alas, alas!

What if you made a pledge of faithfulness for life?
Life itself has no permanence, alas, alas!

The water and air of life taste like poison to me:
that is, it did not agree with you, alas, alas!

What has become of the rose-scattering of the grace of your radiance?
Your tulip-work now blooms on the dust, alas, alas!

For shame of disgrace, to go and hide under the veil of dust!
The keeping of love's secrets ends with you, alas, alas!

The honour of the pledge of love has mixed with the dust:
the ways and customs of friendship are gone from the world, alas, alas!

The hand that tested the sword has gone from its work:
not one deadly wound reached the heart, alas, alas!

How can one pass the dark nights of the rainy season,
when the eye is used to counting stars, alas, alas!
