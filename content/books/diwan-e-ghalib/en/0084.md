The turning of the cup of a hundred coloured radiances is from you;
the holding of the mirror of one bewildered eye is from me.

Asad, a fire drips from the hot glance:
the straw and rubbish of the garden are lit with lamps because of me.

◆

If wildness brings the wish for tearing to nakedness,
like the dawn, the heart's wound would act as a collar.

Your radiance is such that if one thinks of it,
it makes the heart's eye a shrine of bewilderment.

The heart despairs even of breaking: O Lord, how long
must the glass show its heaviness of life against the mountain?

If the tavern were broken by the proud drunken eye,
the hairline crack in the flask would serve as the cup's eyelashes.

Love has had the tresses sign a bond with the down on the cheek:
whatever disorder it makes is wholly accepted.

◆

Let her come in a dream and at least give comfort to my distress,
but first let the heart's burning give me room to sleep.

Finish us just by showing the movement of your lips:
if you will not give a kiss, at least give an answer with your mouth.

Your weeping in tenderness kills:
let anyone else temper the sword of the glance like you.

Cupbearer, if you hate us, pour it into our cupped hands:
if you will not give a cup, do not, but at least give wine.
