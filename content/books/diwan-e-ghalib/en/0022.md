Despair and hope demanded a field of battle;
the weakness of my courage set up the magic of a beggar's heart.

Ghalib, the themes of thirsty longing could not be captured,
though I opened my heart and made even the river a shore.

◆

No speck of earth in the garden is useless:
here even the path is the wick for the tulip's scar.

Without wine, who has the strength for the turmoil of awareness?
The weakness of courage has drawn the marking line on the cup.

The roses laugh at the nightingale's business:
what they call love is a disorder of the brain.

The intoxication of composing verse is not new to me:
I am an old addict of the lamp's smoke.

A hundred times I have been freed from love's bonds,
but what can I do when the heart itself is the enemy of ease?

Without the heart's blood the wave of the glance is dust in the eye:
this tavern is ruined in the search for wine.

Your blossoming garden is a carpet for the heart's delight;
whose mind could the spring cloud's wine cellar match?

◆

She read my hidden grief in the furrows of my brow,
and the secret of my letter in its rambling address.

The mirror is still polished no more than a single *alif* (the straight first letter of the alphabet):
I have been tearing my collar ever since I understood.

Do not ask me to explain why my mind is so captive:
my heart grew so narrow that I took it for a prison.
