In the crush of weakness, how could feebleness show itself?
There is not even room in my body for my stature to bend.

The cupbearer's pride has taken away my ocean-drinking thirst:
today the vein of the wine's wave is not in the flask's neck.

Ghalib, what glory had I at home, that I should be honoured abroad?
Without formality, I am that handful of straw that is not in the furnace.

I could not discharge the duty of praising her grace:
if there is one gesture, I would call it my death.

The rings are wide-open eyes turned toward the heart:
I would call every strand of her tresses a glance lined with collyrium.

I, and a hundred thousand liver-scratching cries;
you, and that one refusal to listen: what can I say?

Tyrant, do not want me shamed by my own suspicion:
alas, God forbid that I should call you faithless!

◆

Open up to us one day at the hour of drinking,
or else one day we will tease you, with drunkenness as our excuse.

Do not be proud of the height of the edifice of the world of possibility:
in the fortunes of this height there is a fall one day.

We drank wine on credit, but we knew that, yes,
our hungry drunkenness would bear fruit one day.

O heart, count even the songs of grief a blessing:
this instrument of being will fall silent one day.
