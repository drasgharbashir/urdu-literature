The qibla of the aim of the entreating glance
is again the curtain of the howdah.

The eye is the broker of the goods of disgrace;
the heart is a buyer of the taste for humiliation.

The same hundred-coloured wearing out of lament,
the same hundredfold raining of tears.

Again, from longing for her graceful walk, the heart
is a Doomsday field of restlessness.

Again the radiance displays its grace:
it is market day for giving up one's life.

Again we are dying for the same faithless one:
again our life is the same.

Again the court of coquetry is open:
the criminal court is busy.

Darkness is falling over the world:
again the tresses are in charge of the records.

Again a piece of the liver has made a plea:
it is one cry, sighing and wailing.

Again the witnesses of love are summoned:
the order for the raining of tears is issued.

The case that was between the heart and the eyelashes
is again being heard today.

Ghalib, this self-forgetting is not without cause:
there is something it is keeping veiled.
