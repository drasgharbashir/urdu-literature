Asad, my revenue came from the scar on my heart;
the fire-temple had not yet become the estate of the salamander.

◆

Last night, when she lit up the private chamber of honour,
the thread of every candle was a thorn in the lantern's robe.

The henna that grows for miles around the lover's tomb:
Lord, how it died longing to kiss her feet!

I saw no fruit of love but the breaking of desire:
heart joined to heart was like one lip of regret.

How can I describe the leisure of grief's sickness?
The heart's blood I drank owed nothing to digestion.

◆

She looked in the mirror and was left with a look of her own:
how proud the lady was of not giving her heart!

Do not behead the messenger with your own hand;
it was not his fault, the fault was mine.

◆

I am no longer fit to offer love's devotion:
the heart I was proud of is that heart no more.

I go carrying the scar of longing for existence;
I am a snuffed candle, no longer fit for the gathering.

O heart, find another way to die, for I
am no longer worthy of the killer's hand and dagger.
