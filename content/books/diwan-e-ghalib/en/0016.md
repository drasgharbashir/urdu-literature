I had to give account of every single drop:
the blood of my heart was a trust held by the beloved's eyelashes.

Now there is only me and the mourning of a whole city of desire:
the mirror you broke was full of images.

Drag my body through the streets, for I
had given my life to the love of the roadside.

Do not ask about the mirage-waves of the desert of faithfulness:
every grain of sand gleamed like the temper of a sword.

I too thought little of love's grief, but now
I find it was what was left when the world's grief diminished.

It is so hard for any task to become easy:
even a human being cannot easily become humane.

Weeping wants the ruin of my dwelling:
wilderness drips from its doors and walls.

Alas for the madness of longing, which every moment makes me
go there myself and then be bewildered myself.

Beauty so demands to be looked at
that even the mirror's grain longs to become eyelashes.

Do not ask about the joy of the killing-ground of those who long:
the baring of the sword is a festival for the eyes.

We took into the dust the scar of longing for joy:
may you be, and blossom in a hundred colours like a garden.

The joy of a piece of the heart is to take the wound of longing;
the pleasure of a sore heart is to be drowned in a salt-cellar.
