If madness does not bear the charge of comfort from happiness,
the pleasure of life is salt sprinkled on the heart's scratch.

What can the effort for freedom do against life's struggles?
The wave of water's leisure to flow became its chain.

Even after death the madman is a shrine for children:
the sparks of stones scattered flowers on my grave.

Reproach is the punishment for one who cries out at the beloved's cruelty:
lest Doomsday's morning should be a laugh that shows the teeth.

The dust of Majnun's desert would give Laila's vein a fibrous root
if the farmer sowed the lancet's point instead of grain.

The moth's wing was perhaps the sail of the boat of wine:
from the gathering's heat the circling cup began to flow.

How can I tell the cruelty of the taste for flying?
The strength of my pinion flew away before it flew.

How long shall I weep behind her tent? It is a calamity!
O Lord, was a wall of stone not in my fate?

Through want of measure we became light in everyone's eyes:
the more we became, the less we were.

The snare was hidden very close to the nest:
we had not yet flown when we were caught.

Our existence is proof of our own annihilation:
we were erased so far that we became our own oath.
