Again I am filling the pen of my eyelashes with the heart's blood,
preparing to make my hem a decorated garden.

Again heart and eye have become each other's rivals,
one equipped with gazing, the other with fancy.

Again the heart goes round the street of reproach,
having laid waste the idol-house of self-conceit.

Again longing is seeking a buyer,
displaying the goods of mind, heart and soul.

Again fancy runs after every rose and tulip,
carrying the glance's stock of a hundred gardens.

Again I want to open the beloved's letter,
having offered my life to the charm of its address.

Again desire asks for someone on the edge of the roof,
her black tresses scattered over her face.

Again longing wants someone face to face,
the dagger of her eyelashes made sharper with collyrium.

Again the glance looks for a new spring of grace,
her face made a garden by the glow of wine.

Again the heart seeks that same leisure, to sit
day and night lost in thinking of the beloved.

Again I have a mind to lie at someone's door,
head bowed under the burden of the doorkeeper's favour.

Ghalib, do not tease us, for again, with the surge of tears,
we sit here ready to raise a storm.

◆

**The ghazals are complete.**
