The rival asks after me in the pain of separation from her,
as freely as if he were a friend who shares my grief.

So that I will know he can reach her there,
he brings me the message of her promise to meet.

When I begin to complain of my weak mind,
he starts telling of the beloved's ambergris-scented tresses.

If he finds me weeping quietly,
he laughs and tells me of the beloved's playful talk.

Should I complain of the enemy's kindnesses,
or give thanks for the pleasure of the beloved's torments?

I love this *ghazal* (lyric poem in couplets) of mine with all my heart,
because, Ghalib, its *radif* (repeated end-word) repeats "friend" so often.

### Radif jim (ج)

The garden is arranged in a new way today:
the dove's ring is a door-knocker today.

With every cry comes a piece of my heart:
the thread of my breath is a lasso to snare effect today.

O peace, step aside! O order, go!
The flood of tears is after the walls and doors today.

◆

Look, we are nursing the one sick with love:
if he does not recover, what can the healer do?
