O heart, my verse is so hard
that, hearing it again and again, the accomplished poets

ask me to say something easy:
if I speak, it is hard; if I do not speak, it is hard. [TN: the last line is in Persian.]

◆

The *dal* (lentils) the King of Jamshid's rank has sent me
is *dal* (evidence) of the Emperor's kindness and favours.

This king-approved dal, beyond argument and dispute,
is *dal* (the letter D) of *daulat*, *din*, *danish* and *dad*: wealth, faith, wisdom and justice.

◆

In the King the attributes of the Lord of Majesty are joined,
the marks of majesty and of beauty together.

Why should high and low not rejoice together?
This year *Shab-e Qadr* (the Night of Power) and *Diwali* (the Hindu festival of lights) fall together.
