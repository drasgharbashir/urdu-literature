Whoever gets a single blade of straw from the rubbish there
would scorn a fan made of a fairy's wing.

The dust of the desert of *Najaf* (where Ali is buried) is the essence of the mystics' journey;
the eye of its footprint is the mirror of waking fortune.

An atom of that dust is the sun's mirror of pride;
the dust of that desert is hope's pilgrim garment of spring.

From there creation seeks the intoxication of grace:
every wave of dust displays the yawn of creation.

By your bounty, O candle of the bedchamber of spring,
the moth's heart is ablaze with lamps, the nightingale's wing pomegranate-red.

Like a peacock the hall of mirrors takes flight
in the longing for your radiance, in the desire to see you.

From grief for your descendants, on the face of the sky
the new moon in the string of stars is a pearl-raining eyelash.

We, for worship, have your footprint as the prayer-stone;
we, for discipline, take strength from your courage.

Hidden in your praise is the song in praise of the Prophet:
from your cup the wine of the surge of mysteries is plain.

The grain of the mirror of the praying hand, that is, its effect,
is on one side the pride of the eyelashes, on the other the grief of the thorn.

Let the pupil make the eye's good fortune a house of mourning
if the eye does not hold up a mirror to the dust of your door.

For the enemy of the Prophet's family, in the world's house of joy,
let the niche of the wall display the yawn of the flood.

Asad, from eye to heart, is the mirror of one ray of longing:
the line of the writer's cup is brimming with the bounty of meaning.
