There, the show of roses had lit up the stream with lamps;
here, pure blood flowed from the lashes of my wet eyes.

Here, my troubled head was a wall-breaker from sleeplessness;
there, her proud head was lost in a pillow of brocade.

Here, my breath lit the candle of a gathering of self-forgetting;
there, the show of roses spread a carpet for a company of friends.

There, from floor to heaven, was a storm of waves of colour;
here, from earth to sky, was a chapter of burning.

Suddenly, in this manner, it began to drip pure blood:
the heart that had delighted in the probing of the fingernail.

Last night my heart's lament lacked any sign of effect:
it was rue burnt in the rival's gathering of union, restless though it was.

How joyful the heart is at the coming of the flood!
The lover's house had been only an instrument for the sound of water.

What can I say of my pride in the days I sat in ashes?
The side of my thought was given over to a bed of sable fur.

My own madness, unable to reach its goal, did nothing; otherwise here
every particle could rival the world-lighting sun.

Why do you not care for your captives today?
Until yesterday your heart too was a chapter of kindness and faithfulness.

Remember the days when every ring of your net
was a sleepless eye waiting for prey.

Last night I held Ghalib back; otherwise you would have seen
the heavens become the foam on the flood of his weeping.
