Asad, my hands and feet swelled with joy
when she said, "Just press my feet a little."

◆

From my fever every thread of the bedding is given over to struggle:
my head is a pain to the pillow, my body a burden to the bed.

The tear that ran off to the desert is the light of the hem's eyes;
the heart, fallen without hand or foot, is the bed's darling child.

How fortunate my illness! You have come to visit me:
the candle's glow at my pillow is the bed's waking fortune.

In the storm of the surge of anxiety on the evening of loneliness,
the ray of the sun of Doomsday morning is a thread of the bed.

The scent of her musky tresses still comes from the pillow:
to our sight, *Zulaikha's* (the lover of Joseph) dream is a shame to the bed.

Ghalib, what can I say of the state of my heart in the beloved's absence?
From restlessness every thread of the bed is a thorn of the bed.

◆

There is danger that the thread of love may become the jugular vein:
the pride of friendship is a calamity; may you not become an enemy.

Ghalib, take it as a shortfall of growth in this season
if the rose does not become a robe for the cypress's stature.
