Frenzy put the black spot on my heart in order:
it became clear that the scar's only capital was smoke.

In a dream my fancy had dealings with you;
when my eyes opened there was neither loss nor gain.

I still take lessons in the school of the heart's grief,
but only this: "it went" and "it was".

The shroud covered the stains of my nakedness;
otherwise, in every garment, I was a disgrace to existence.

Asad, the mountain-digger could not die without his axe:
he was dazed by the hangover of rites and restraints.

You say, "We will not give back the heart if we find it lying about."
Where is a heart to lose? We have found what we sought.

Through love my nature found the taste of living:
I found the cure for pain, and found a pain that has no cure.

Simplicity and cunning, abandon and alertness:
I found beauty testing courage through its indifference.

The bud began to open again; today we saw our heart
slain in blood, and found it lost.

I do not know the state of the heart, only this much:
I searched for it many times, and you found it many times.

The uproar of the counsellor's advice sprinkled salt on the wound;
someone should ask him what pleasure he got from it.

He is a friend of my enemy! So much for trusting the heart:
I found my sighs ineffective and my laments unable to reach.
