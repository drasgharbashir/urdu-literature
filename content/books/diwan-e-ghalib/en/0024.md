What a quarrel there will be with *Rizwan* (the keeper of paradise)
if I remember your house in heaven!

What desolation, desolation itself!
Seeing the wilderness, I remembered my home.

Asad, as a boy I once raised a stone at *Majnun*,
then I remembered my own head.

◆

Dry-lipped in thirst, I am the shrine of the dead,
a place of pilgrimage for the sorrow-hearted.

All hopelessness, all suspicion,
I am the heart of those deceived by faithfulness.

◆

Tyrant, you were never anyone's friend;
others suffer cruelty such as I never suffered.

The hand of fate let me go like the moon of Nakhshab (a false moon made by an impostor),
though the sun had not yet become its equal.

Ability has always matched courage:
in my eyes is the drop that never became a pearl.

Until I saw the sight of the beloved's stature
I did not believe in the turmoil of Resurrection Day.

Simple-hearted, I am glad the beloved is displeased,
since the lesson of longing had not been repeated.

The sea of sins dried up from its shallowness:
not even the hem of my robe had become wet.
