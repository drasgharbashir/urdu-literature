Detachment is no pretext for estrangement:
shun neither yourself nor others, even in wildness.

Asad, we will not rise now from the door of that troublemaker,
even if Doomsday falls on our head there.

There she is seized with dread of heart, and here I am ashamed:
that is, let it not be from the effect of my sigh.

Look not at yourself but at the taste for cruelty:
so that the mirror is not the eye of the prey.

◆

In his simplicity the mountain-digger gave his life; I fall at his feet.
Alas, why did the old woman's feet not break? [TN: in the legend an old woman, sent by Khusrau, falsely told *Farhad* that Shirin was dead.]

When I wash that silver-bodied one's feet to drink the water,
out of stubbornness she pulls her feet out of the basin.

We ran away a great deal, and this is the punishment for it:
made captive, we press the robber's feet.

Since I wandered far and wide in search of ointment,
that worn-out body's feet are more wounded than the body.

God, what a taste for desert wandering, that after death
my feet move of themselves inside the shroud.

Such is the surge of roses in spring that everywhere
the garden birds' feet catch as they fly.

The poor fellow has come so far, Shaikh-ji:
why should we not press the Brahmin's feet in the Kaaba?
