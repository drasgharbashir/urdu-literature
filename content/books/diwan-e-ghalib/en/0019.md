Who ever claimed to be stout-hearted?
What is the patience of a lover's heart worth?

Why this murderous promise that tests one's patience?
Why this faithless mischief that robs one of strength?

Ghalib, her every word is a torment to the soul:
her phrasing, her hints, her graces!

◆

Asad, I am a beggar running mad, without head or foot,
my back-scratcher is the claw of a gazelle's eyelashes.

◆

As an offering to grace, my gift is the shame of falling short:
my claim to purity is rolling in blood in a hundred colours.

Let not beauty that loves to be seen be shamed by faithlessness:
the claim to purity is sealed with a hundred glances.

Pay the alms due on beauty, O radiance of insight, so that, like the sun,
the begging bowl becomes the lamp of the dervish's house.

Careless one, you did not kill me knowingly, though I was guiltless; yet on your neck
the debt of our acquaintance stays like innocent blood.

The tongue's longing is lost in giving thanks for speechlessness,
which wiped out the demand to complain of helplessness.

It is the same thing that is breath here and the scent of the rose there:
the garden's radiance is the reason for my colourful song.

The mouth of every scolding beauty is a chain of disgrace:
faithless one, your faithlessness is talked of even in the realm of non-existence.
