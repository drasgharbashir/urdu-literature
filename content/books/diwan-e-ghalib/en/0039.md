### Radif shin (ش)

If the straw of the mirror's grain does not take freshness from the down of her cheek,
the beloved's face would set fire to the house of the mirror.

The lover's difficulty is solved by the glow of beauty:
the thorn would not come out of the candle's side unless fire drew it out.

### Radif ain (ع)

At evening the ray is the thread of the sun's path:
the heavens open the embrace of farewell with the new moon.

◆

From the beloved's face the candle has its eternal burning:
the fire of the rose has become the candle's water of life.

In the tongue of those who know the tongue, silence is death:
this was made clear in the gathering by the candle's speech.

At the flame's hint it finishes the whole tale:
the candle tells its story in the manner of the annihilated.

O flame, it grieves for the moth's longing:
your trembling shows the candle's weakness.

At the thought of you the soul quivers
like the wind's shimmer and the candle's fluttering wings.

Do not ask about the spring of the joy of love's scar:
its blooming is martyred by the candle's autumn flower.
