Do not draw your breath outside the gathering of longing:
if there is no wine, wait for the cup.

Do not ask how ardent my search to see you is:
draw the grain from my mirror like a thorn.

Waiting, O heart, is your pretext for rest:
who signalled you to lie back on the bed?

The narcissus looks at you with longing:
to spite the rival's blind heart and eye, drink the cup.

With half a wink, repay what coquetry entrusted to you:
draw the dagger from the sheath that veils my heart's wound.

In my cup is the wine of a hidden fire:
on the tablecloth, serve the kebab of the salamander's heart.

### Radif dal (د)

After me, beauty was freed from the strain of flirting:
at last the cruel are at rest after me.

No one is left fit for the office of love-madness:
airs and graces were dismissed after me.

When the candle goes out, smoke rises from it:
the flame of love put on black after me.

The essence of cruelty has no one to show itself to:
the proud glance is angry with collyrium after me.

For the mad, madness is an embrace of farewell:
the collar parts from the shirt after me.

Who can match love's man-felling wine?
The cupbearer's lips repeat the call again and again after me.
