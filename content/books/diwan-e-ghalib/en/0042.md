Congratulations on the breeze's freedom, for on every side
lie broken the rings of the net of longing for the rose.

Whatever was, died deceived by the wave of colour:
alas for the lament of the blood-lipped, singing rose!

Happy is that black-drunk companion who,
like the rose's shadow, keeps his head at the rose's feet.

Spring creates it for your sake:
my rival is the perfumed breath of the rose.

They keep me ashamed before the spring breeze:
the flask without wine and the heart without longing for the rose.

Before the majesty of your jealous beauty's radiance
the colour of the rose's grace is blood in my eyes.

It is the illusion of your radiance alone that even today
the rose runs helplessly after the rose.

Ghalib, I long to embrace the one
whose thought is the rose in the pocket of the rose's robe.

### Radif mim (م)

The free do not grieve for more than a single breath:
we light the candle of the house of mourning with lightning.

The gambler of fancy breaks up whole gatherings:
we are the turning of the cards of a single idol-house's magic.

In spite of a world of uproar we are not born at all:
we are the lamps of the bedchamber of the moth's heart.

This giving up of the search is from weakness, not contentment:
we are a burden on the cushion of manly courage.
