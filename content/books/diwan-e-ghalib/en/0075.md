See the riches of Majnun's blood-raining eye:
a whole desert of roses' radiance is the mat before his feet.

◆

If the flame does not stand guard over the cash of the heart's scar,
then chill lies hidden in the ambush of speechlessness.

What can I expect from her in the time of youth,
who never heard my story even in childhood?

It is not good to cause anyone pain for nothing, or else I would say:
"O Lord, may my enemy be granted my life."

◆

In my house of darkness the night of grief is surging:
one candle, the sign of dawn, and that too is out.

Neither news of union nor the sight of beauty:
it is long since eye and ear made peace.

Martyred in love, I have found a thousand bodies:
every wave of the road's dust is a shoulder for my head.

Wine has unveiled self-adorning beauty:
O longing, here there is leave to surrender the senses.

To see the pearl on the necklace of the fair ones!
How high stands the star of the pearl-seller!

Sight is the wine, courage the cupbearer, the glance drunk:
the gathering of fancy is a tavern without noise.

O newcomers to the carpet of the heart's desire,
beware, if you long for flute and drink!
