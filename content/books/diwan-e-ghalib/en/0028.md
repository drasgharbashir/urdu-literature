From you, in my fate, like a combination lock,
it was written that we part the moment things come right.

The heart was spent in the struggle to treat its pain:
in the rubbing, the knot's chance of opening was worn away.

Now we are denied even cruelty; good God,
to become such an enemy of the faithful!

From weakness, weeping turned into cold sighs:
now we believe that water can turn to air.

For the thought of your hennaed fingers to leave my heart
is like the nail being torn from the flesh.

For me it is like the spring cloud raining and then clearing:
to vanish, weeping, in the grief of separation.

If the scent of the rose does not long for your street,
why does it become the dust of the breeze's wandering path?

Ghalib, the show of roses gives a taste for seeing:
the eye should open to every colour.

So that the miracle of the polishing air becomes clear to you,
see how in the rains the mirror turns green.

### Radif be (ب)

The time has come again for the wave of wine to spread its wings,
to give the wine-duck heart and hands for swimming.

Do not ask why the people of the garden are so drunk:
in the shade of the vine the air becomes a wave of wine.

Whoever drowns in wine has good fortune:
even when it passes over his head, the wave of wine is the wing of the *huma* (a bird of good omen).
