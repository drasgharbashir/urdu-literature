Entreaty's spell is no rival for a hard request:
may the prayer be granted, O Lord, that Khizr's life be long.

Do not wander aimlessly through the desert of the illusion of being:
even now there are ups and downs in your imagining.

Union with the radiance is a spectacle, but where is the mind
to give wings to the mirror of waiting?

Every lover's atom worships the sun:
even turned to dust, it did not lose its longing for the radiance of grace.

Ghalib, do not ask how wide is the tavern of madness,
where this bowl of the heavens is a single dustpan.

See the reach of generosity's effort: over the whole earth
the pearl-raining cloud still passes with blistered feet.

The page of the desert is wholly burnt paper:
in the footprint there still burns the heat of the walk.

I am neither the flower of song nor the veil of the lute:
I am the sound of my own breaking.

You, and the dressing of your curling locks;
I, and far-off, long anxieties.

Boasting of dignity is the deceit of a simple heart:
here we are, with secrets that melt the breast.

I am the captive of love for the hunter,
or else I still have strength to fly.
