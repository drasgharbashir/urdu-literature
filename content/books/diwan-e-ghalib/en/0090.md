Since the practice of gazing is a sign of madness,
the opening and shutting of the eyelashes is the slap of shame.

I do not know how the scar of the taunt of broken promises can be erased
for you, for whom even the mirror is a whirlpool of reproach.

Do not break the thread of safety in the twists and turns of lust:
the helpless glance is the thread's end of safety.

Faithfulness opposed, the claim of love without foundation,
feigned madness and the season of roses: what a calamity!

◆

Through weeping we grew bolder in love:
we were washed so well that we became quite clean.

The tools of drinking went to pay the price of wine:
these were the only two accounts, and so they were settled.

Though you were disgraced in the world by your wandering,
at least your wits grew sharp.

Who says the nightingale's lament has no effect?
Behind the veil of the rose, lakhs of livers were torn.

What do you ask about the existence and non-existence of the people of longing?
They became the straw and rubbish of their own fire.

We went to complain to her of her neglect:
she gave one glance, and we turned to dust.

Yesterday she lifted Asad's body in such a way
that even the enemies grieved to see it.
