In this world the mark of faithfulness brought no comfort;
it is a word that was never fulfilled by a meaning.

The green down of your cheek could not subdue your unruly curls:
even this emerald was no match for the serpent's breath.

I wanted to be freed from the sorrow of faithfulness;
that tyrant would not agree even to my dying.

Let my heart at least be a path for thoughts of wine and cup,
if my breath could not be the road to piety's goal.

I am content even with your not making a promise, for my ear
never owed a debt to the song of comfort.

Whom should I blame for my bad luck?
I wanted to die, and even that did not happen.

Ghalib died from the shock of a single movement of the lips:
so weak was he that he could not bear even the breath of Jesus.

◆

The ascetic so praises the garden of *Rizwan* (paradise),
yet it is only a bouquet on the forgotten shelf of us who are lost to ourselves.

How can I describe the cruelty of the probing eyelashes?
Each drop of my blood is a bead in a rosary of coral.

Even the killer's might could not stop my laments:
the straw I held in my teeth became a fibre of the reed-bed.

I will show you a spectacle if time gives me the chance:
every scar on my heart is the seed of a cypress of lamps.
