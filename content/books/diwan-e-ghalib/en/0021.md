I know it: you, and answer a letter!
Yet I am a victim of my love of writing.

If this is spring, then spring is henna on autumn's feet:
the world's pleasure is lasting heaviness of heart.

In the grief of parting do not trouble me to stroll in the garden;
I have no patience for the flowers' pointless laughter.

I still long to be intimate with beauty,
though every pore of my body does the work of a seeing eye.

We gave her our heart before she ever showed her airs and graces;
what patience have we for beauty's demands?

Do not say that weeping matches the heart's longing:
in my eyes is the whole income and spending of the ocean.

Asad, I look at the sky and remember her:
in its cruelty there is something of her way of ruling.

The drop of wine, from wonder, began to breathe,
so that the rim line of the wine cup became a string of pearls.

See how the credit of love ruins one's house:
the rival sighed, but she grew angry with me.
