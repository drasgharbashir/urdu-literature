It burns when it sees me at the beloved's pillow:
why should my heart not bear the scar of the candle's suspicion?

### Radif fe (ف)

For fear of the rival we do not bid our senses farewell:
alas, O free will, we have become so helpless here!

The heart burns that we did not burn once and for all:
alas for the incompleteness of the fire-raining breath!

### Radif kaf (ک)

Where would heedless children sprinkle salt on the wound?
What a taste it would be if even stone held salt!

The dust of the beloved's path is what the heart's wound prides itself on:
otherwise, how much salt is there in the world?

May there be plenty for me, and may it be blessed for you:
the pain of the nightingale's lament, and the salt of the rose's laughter.

Whose tumult of galloping was on the seashore, that today
salt lies round the shore on the wound of the river's wave?

She praises the wound of my liver, bravo, bravo!
She remembers me wherever she sees salt.

It is a pity to leave the lover's wounded body:
the heart asks for wounds, and the limbs beg for salt.

I will not beg a stranger's favour to increase my pain:
the wound, like the killer's smile, is salt from head to foot.

Ghalib, do you remember those days when in the ecstasy of taste
I gathered with my eyelashes the salt that fell from the wound?
