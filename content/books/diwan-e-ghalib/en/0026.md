The mirror's door is open on all six sides:
here there is no longer any difference between flawed and perfect.

Longing has untied the strings of beauty's veil:
nothing now stands between us but the gaze.

Though I stayed pledged to the cruelties of the times,
I was never unmindful of thoughts of you.

The wish to sow faithfulness has left my heart, for there
no harvest remains but longing for a harvest.

I do not fear love's cruelty, but, Asad,
the heart I was proud of is that heart no more.

◆

Jealousy says, "Alas for her sincerity to the rival!"
Reason says, "That heartless one, whose friend is she?"

Every particle is a cup in the tavern of enchantment:
Majnun's wandering knows the winks of Laila.

Longing furnishes the pride of the helpless:
the speck knows the desert's wealth, the drop knows the ocean.

I, and that piece of calamity, my wild heart, which is
the enemy of peace and the friend of wandering.

We should not complain of jealousy of each other:
my knee is my companion, and the mirror your friend.

Asad, the mountain-digger painted only one image of *Shirin*:
dashing his head on stone, he still found no friend.
