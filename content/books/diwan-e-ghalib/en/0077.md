At least you have pledged to kill me:
alas, if the pledge is not firm!

Ghalib, you have sworn to give up wine:
there is no trusting your oath.

◆

From the crowd of grief my head is bent so low
that it is hard to tell the thread of my hem from the thread of my gaze.

The aim in mending the wound is the pleasure of the needle's wound:
do not think the madman is careless of respecting pain.

Ghalib, in the garden where that rose shows her radiance,
the bursting of the rosebud is the sound of the heart's laughter.

◆

Since I wander the desert, I have drawn my feet into my hem:
the thorns in my feet are the grain of the mirror of my knee.

See the state of my heart at the time of embrace:
every tip of your hair is a familiar glance to me.

I am wholly the instrument of the tune of complaint; do not ask:
it is best that you do not touch me in front of people.

◆

In the gathering where you speak with grace,
life would come into the body of a wall's picture.

Cypress and pine would follow you like a shadow
if you came into the garden with that charming stature.
