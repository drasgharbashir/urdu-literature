Heedlessness is surety for life, and Asad guarantor of delight:
O sudden death, what are you waiting for?

I too am a spectator of desire's magic:
it does not matter whether the aim is ever achieved.

◆

In silence the spectacle of grace emerges:
your glance comes from the heart like collyrium.

From the pressure of solitude's narrowness dew is formed
when the breeze slips into the bud's veil.

Do not ask the lover's breast about the temper of the glance's sword,
for air comes out of the wound of the door's chink.

◆

In the crowd of laments, bewilderment cannot utter a single cry:
silence holds a straw in its teeth from the roots of a hundred reed-beds.

Formality aside, the kindness of the ill-tempered is more deadly:
grace's unveiled glance is a sharp, naked sword.

From the abundance of grief the savour of joy is so lost
that the morning of *Eid* (the festival) is worse to me than a torn collar.

Bring heart and faith in cash if you want to trade with the cupbearer,
for in this market the cup is goods passed from hand to hand.

Grief rears the lover in the embrace of calamity:
our bright lamp is the coral of the ocean of the gale.
