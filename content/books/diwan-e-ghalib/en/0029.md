This rainy season is such a time that it is no wonder if
the bounty of the air turns the wave of being into a wave of wine.

Wave upon wave rises on every side from the storm of delight:
the wave of roses, the wave of sunset, the wave of the breeze, the wave of wine.

However much the spirit of plants thirsts for its pride,
the wave of wine comforts it with the breath of the water of life.

Running through the vine's vein, turning to blood again and again,
the wave of wine spreads its wings with the plumes of colour.

The path of fancy is lit with lamps by the wave of roses,
so brightly does the wave of wine shine in the imagination.

Behind the veil of intoxication it is lost in watching the mind,
so much does the wave of wine aim to grow and flourish.

The intoxication of the season storms over a whole world,
from the wave of the new grass to the wave of wine.

The season of roses explains the tumult of drunkenness, how fine!
It guides the drop to the river: how lovely, the wave of wine!

Asad, my senses fly away at the sight of the roses:
the time has come again for the wave of wine to spread its wings.

### Radif te (ت)

Alas that the heavens made food for teeth [TN: some editions read *didan*, "worms"]
of the fingers of those fit to tie strings of pearls.

Your not giving me the ring is token enough:
at parting you showed me your finger bare.

Asad, from the burning of my heart I write such fiery words
that no one can lay a finger on my letters.
