The ear parted from her message, the eye deprived of her beauty:
one heart, and on top of that this hopelessness, alas, alas!

Ghalib, love had not yet taken on the colour of wildness:
whatever taste for disgrace remained stayed in the heart, alas, alas!

◆

In bewilderment I despair of the world of being:
give comfort the good news that there is hope of dying.

She does not ask after my wandering heart:
she still thinks it is with me.

How far can I describe the delight of grief's fever?
Every hair on my body is a tongue of thanks.

In the pride of beauty she is a stranger to faithfulness,
though she has a heart that knows the truth.

Drink as much wine as you can get on a moonlit night:
heat alone suits this phlegmatic temper.

Asad, every dwelling has its honour from its dweller:
since Majnun died, the forest is sad.

◆

If the use of silence is to hide one's state,
I am glad that my words are impossible to understand.

To whom can I complain of the longing to express?
The heart is the ledger of the income and spending of mute tongues.

O God, behind what veil is the polisher of the mirror?
Mercy is what pleads for the lip that asks nothing.
