Your radiance so transformed the hall of mirrors
as the sun's reflection turns the world into a field of dew.

A form of ruin lies hidden in my very building:
the lightning that burns the harvest is made of the farmer's warm blood.

Grass has sprung up all over the house; behold the desolation:
my doorkeeper's living now depends on digging out grass.

Hidden in my silence are a hundred thousand longings turned to blood;
I am a dead lamp, voiceless, on a stranger's grave.

A reflection of the image of the beloved still remains:
my withered heart is like Joseph's prison cell.

You must be sleeping in a rival's arms somewhere today; otherwise
why would you come to my dream with secret smiles?

Who knows whose blood has turned to water?
It is a calamity that your eyelashes are wet with tears.

Ghalib, the path to annihilation is always before my eyes,
for it is the binding that holds the scattered pages of the world together.

◆

A whole desert of weariness will not lessen my zeal:
my footprint is a bubble on the wave of my going.

I once loved the garden, but now I am so out of sorts
that a waft of rose scent leaves me gasping.

◆

Wholly pledged to love, yet bound to the love of existence,
I worship the lightning and grieve for the harvest.
