Why should I not give a mirror, that it may be called a spectacle?
Where can I bring one from that could be called like you?

Longing has placed in your gathering of fancy
what might be called the bouquet of the glance of the heart's black core.

O God, who has breathed into love's ear
the spell of waiting, which might be called desire?

Throw upon your head, from the crowd of a stranger's pain,
that one handful of dust which might be called the desert.

Hidden in the wet eye by the longing to see you
is a longing that has broken its reins, which might be called a river.

For the flowers of pleasure to bloom there is needed
a spring morning which might be called the cotton plug of the flask.

Ghalib, do not mind if the preacher speaks ill of you:
is there anyone whom everyone calls good?

The dew on the tulip is not empty of grace:
the scar of the painless heart is the place where shyness looks.

The heart is turned to blood by the struggle of longing to see her:
the mirror is in the hand of the idol drunk on henna.

What the longing for flame did could not be done by flame:
how much the soul has burned at the heart's dejection!

There is such playfulness in your image that with a hundred delights
the mirror opens its arms like a rose.
