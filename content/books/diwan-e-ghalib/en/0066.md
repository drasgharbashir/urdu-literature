Do not say to me, "You used to call me your life":
these days my heart is weary even of life.

I have drawn the picture of an eye on the heading of the letter,
so that it may be clear to you that it longs to see you.

◆

My existence is the open space of the bewildered dwelling of desire:
what they call lament is the *anqa* of that world.

What is autumn, and what do they call the season of roses? Whatever the season,
we are the same, there is the cage, and there is mourning for wings and feathers.

The faithfulness of beloveds is by chance; otherwise, friend,
who has seen the plaint of sorrowing hearts take effect?

The playfulness of thought could not bear the pain of despair:
rubbing the palm in regret is a pledge to renew desire.

◆

If it is not love in me, let it be wildness:
let my wildness be your fame.

Do not break off ties with us:
if there is nothing else, let there be enmity.

What disgrace is there in my being there?
O you, if it is not the gathering, let it be solitude.

We are not our own enemies either:
let the stranger love you, then.

Whatever comes, let it come from our own existence:
if not awareness, then let it be heedlessness.
