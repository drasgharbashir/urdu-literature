Who can praise the one whom God has praised?
Who can adorn the highest paradise?

Asadullah Asad is goods in the market of sins,
for whom there is no buyer but you.

In the boldness of setting out his wishes he is insolent in asking,
so great is his trust in the courage of your grace.

Grant my prayer such a rank of gracious acceptance
that at every word the answer says "Amen" a hundred times.

Let my breast be so full of grief for *Shabbir* (Husain, Ali's son, martyred at Karbala)
that my eyes stay coloured with the liver's blood.

Let my nature have such ardent longing in love of *Duldul* (Ali's mule)
that as far as it walks, I follow with my brow where it sets its foot.

A heart descended from love, a breast as wide as the faith in one God,
a glance that worships radiance, a breath that chooses truth.

Let the effect of hell's flame and smoke be spent on the enemies;
let the roses and hyacinths of highest paradise be given to the friends.

### Qit'as

*Qit'a* ("fragment"): a short poem of linked couplets that does not open with a rhyming couplet.

**A qit'a showing a charming way of speaking, and easing the sorrow of regret in the beloved's heart [TN: heading in Persian]**

Gone are the days when, not knowing, you would speak
of strangers' faithfulness, and I would keep silent.

Now that it has gone wrong, why be ashamed? Let it go, make peace:
you can have my oath that I will not even say, "Why didn't I tell you?"
