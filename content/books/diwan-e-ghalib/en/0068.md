Ghalib, since our life has passed in this way,
what shall we remember, that we too had a God?

◆

The pace of life is a journey through the road of anxiety:
for the reckoning of this year, the sun is lightning.

The wine flask is the cypress of spring's delight:
the pheasant's wing is the radiance of the wine's wave.

The heel of the foot of steadfastness is wounded:
there is no way to flee, nor strength to stay.

The six directions are the estate of the drinkers' wine-drinking:
the heedless one supposes that the world is ruined.

How can gazing rival that lightning of beauty
whose radiance is veiled by the surge of spring?

What am I, luckless, to do to comfort my heart?
Granted that the glance succeeds in reaching your face.

Asad passed away from the joy of the beloved's message:
I envy the messenger his questions and answers.

◆

See my fate, that I come to envy myself:
that I should look at her, when can I bear to look?

Wash your hands of the heart if there is such heat in thought:
the glass melts from the fierceness of the wine.

O Lord, how can she stop the stranger's insolence?
Even when shyness comes to her, she only blushes.
