My heart burned recklessly with a hidden fire,
like a silent flame, it burned as if it spoke.

No taste for union, no memory of the beloved, is left in my heart:
such a fire struck this house that all it held is burned.

I am beyond even non-existence; otherwise, careless one, many times
the wing of the *anqa* has burned from my fiery sigh.

How can I describe the heat of thought's essence?
A thought of madness crossed my mind, and the desert burned.

Otherwise I would show you a springtime of scars, but I have no heart:
what can I do with this lamplit festival when its master has burned?

I am left with nothing but a longing for coldness, Ghalib, for my heart
burned at the sight of the world's way of showing warmth.

◆

Longing in every guise proves the rival of what one possesses:
*Qais* stayed naked even behind the curtain of a painting.

The wound did not do justice to my narrow heart, O Lord:
even the arrow left the victim's breast fluttering its feathers.

The scent of the rose, the heart's lament, the smoke of the gathering's lamp:
whatever left your gathering left in disarray.

My longing heart was a feast of the pleasure of pain;
my friends' share was only as much as their lips and teeth could take.

O novice of self-annihilation, whose courage loves what is hard!
How very difficult that this task, too, turned out easy.
