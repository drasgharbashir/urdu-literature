# Mir Amman's Petition

Submitted to the gentlemen who govern the College.

May God keep safe the noble gentlemen who value men of good family. On hearing the order in the public notice, this exile, with a thousand pains and labours, made the tale of the four dervishes into *Bagh-o-Bahar* (The Garden and the Spring) in the language of the exalted *Urdu* (the speech of the imperial city of Delhi). By God's grace it has grown green because all you gentlemen have walked through it. Now I hope that I too may receive its fruit, so that the bud of my heart may open like a rose. As the sage Firdausi said in the *Shahnama* (the Persian Book of Kings):

"Great pains I suffered in these thirty years;
I brought Persia back to life with this Persian." [TN: Persian couplet quoted from Firdausi.]

So I, adorning the language of Urdu,
have turned Bengal into Hindustan.

My lords, you know the worth of things; there is no need to set out my request.

O God, may the star of their good fortune go on shining.
