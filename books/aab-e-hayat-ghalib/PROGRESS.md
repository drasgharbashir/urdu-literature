## Source
- Author: Muhammad Husain Azad (1830-1910). Aab-e-Hayat first published Lahore 1880. Public domain (author died 1910, first published before 1931); us_status not needed.
- Urdu text: archive.org `abihayat00azaduoft` (University of Toronto, Robarts Library), "Ab-i hayat", Lucknow, Ihsan Book Depot, 1900. 692 PDF pages; the scan runs in reverse order. Ghalib section = printed pp. 625-670 (PDF pages 64 down to 19), in "Aab-e-Hayat ka panchwan daur".

## Chapters
1 About this edition + Introduction (new); 2 Ghalib heading and life (p625-630); 3-4 "Mirza Sahib ke halat aur tab'i adat" (split; "(جاری)" heading added by this edition); 5-6 "Tasnifat-e Farsi" with letters, sehras, lata'if, death chronogram (split, same); 7 Ghazals (10 ghazals, 92 couplets); 8 Glossary (new, 37 entries).
Azad's footnotes collected per chapter under "# حواشی" / "# Notes". All 8 chapters: ur/en block parity checked (tools/check_lang_parity.py and the thread's own checker).
