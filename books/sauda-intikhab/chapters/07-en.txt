# Quatrain 1. Sauda, Greatness in Poetry Is Yours

Sauda, greatness in poetry is yours;
the robe of speech came to you from the Throne.
The world took you for a prophet in this art;
the ignorant worshipped you as God.

# Quatrain 2. Do Not Cut Your Life Short

Do not cut your life short in the worship of wine;
reach out boldly to your locks.
If there is no Saqi, today that cloud is the wine:
drink water upon water, and be drunk on hunger.

# Quatrain 3. What If I Wept Before the Friend

What if I wept before the friend?
What if I threaded pieces of my heart on my lashes?
It is doubtful these seeds of tears will turn green;
what if I sowed seed in this salt ground?

# Quatrain 4. O Shaikh, You Go Back and Forth to the Haram

O Shaikh, you go back and forth to the Haram;
this circling is a weaver's warp and weft.
I wonder how he will know Him there,
who did not know Him in the Haram of the heart.
