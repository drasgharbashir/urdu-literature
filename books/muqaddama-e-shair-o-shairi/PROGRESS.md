# Muqaddama-e-Shair-o-Shairi (muqaddama-e-shair-o-shairi)

Produced by the cloud project thread "University-ready book list" (queue: /mnt/project-files/notes/book-queue.md, item 4).

## Source
- Author: Khwaja Altaf Husain Hali (1837-1914). First published 1893 (Delhi) as the preface to his Urdu diwan. Public domain everywhere; us_status not needed (pre-1931 work).
- Urdu text: archive.org `dli.ernet.446421` ("Muqadma Ma Dewaan-e-haali", 1893, Delhi press; Allama Iqbal Library, University of Kashmir copy). Page map: book p = scan image index - 4. Text ends p228.
- Omitted: the calligrapher's colophon (p228) and handwritten marginal poet names in the scanned copy (pp93, 169). Hali's own footnotes are kept (chapter-end notes, "Hali's note:").

## Chapters
1 About this edition + Introduction (new); 2-12 Hali's text, divided at the printed main headings (2 Preamble/praise and blame of poetry; 3 unrefined ages; 4 reform; 5 conditions; 6 qualities; 7 Hebrew and Arabic poetry; 8 progress of Urdu poetry; 9-10 ghazal; 11 qasida and marsiya; 12 masnavi); 13 Glossary (new, 49 entries).
