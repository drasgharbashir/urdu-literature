# Mirat-ul-Uroos: progress

**Status:** complete. All 20 site chapters are transcribed and translated (the whole novel, Nazir Ahmad's preface, introduction and chapters I–XXXI).

## Sources (both free downloads on archive.org)

- **Base text:** *The Bride's Mirror; or, Mir-atu l-arus*, ed. G. E. Ward, London: Henry Frowde, 1899. Urdu in Roman letters. https://archive.org/details/McGillLibrary-122914-2109
- **Check copy:** Urdu lithograph, Delhi, 1912 (Digital Library of India). https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.409512

The author died in 1912 and the novel was first published in 1869, so it is in the public domain in Pakistan, India and the US. Ward's notes, running headers and English translation are not used, and his corrections to the text were not adopted.

## Method

- Ward's Roman Urdu was transcribed back into Urdu script page by page (modern spelling). Doubtful words and lines lost at page tops were checked against the 1912 lithograph images.
- English is a new, faithful translation, block for block with the Urdu (same number of blocks and of lines per block).
- Persian verse and quotations are translated, with the original in a `[TN: …]` note.
- Chapter titles are editorial: the original chapters are untitled.
