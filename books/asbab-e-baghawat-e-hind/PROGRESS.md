## Source
- Urdu text: Asbab-e-Baghawat-e-Hind 1857, Munshi Fazluddin, tajir-e-kutub, Qaumi Kutub Khana, Bazar Kashmiri, Lahore; undated, printed after 1898 (calls the author "marhum"); library stamp "Checked 1973".
  Internet Archive: https://archive.org/details/AsbabEBaghawatEHind-SirSyedAhmadKhan (65 PDF pages; text = pp. 3-58, Persian proclamation = pp. 59-62).
  Transcribed from page images (220 dpi), page by page.
- Omitted: title page, publisher's list of works (p. 2), advertisement for Khutbat-e-Ahmadiya (p. 63), page numbers, marginal side-headings.
  Two printer's dittographies removed (noted in ch1). Edmond's letter, which Sir Syed refers to, is not printed in this edition (TN in ch4).
- Author died 1898; first printed 1859: public domain everywhere. No modern editorial matter. us_status: not applicable.

## Chapters
1 About this edition + Introduction (new); 2-8 Sir Syed's text split at his own headings (preface/false causes, root cause, causes 1-5);
9 Appendix: the Persian proclamation of the Lt-Governor (نقل اشتہار) with English translation; 10 Glossary (new).
