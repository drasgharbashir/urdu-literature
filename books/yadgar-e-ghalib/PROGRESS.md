# Yadgar-e-Ghalib, Part One (yadgar-e-ghalib)

Produced by the cloud project thread "University-ready book list" (queue: /mnt/project-files/notes/book-queue.md).

## Source
- Author: Khwaja Altaf Husain Hali (1837-1914). First published 1897, in his lifetime. Public domain (author died 1914, published before 1931); us_status not needed.
- Urdu text: archive.org `YadgarGhalib`, the first edition: "مرتبہ خاکسار الطاف حسین حالی پانی پتی، سنہ ۱۸۹۷ء، محمد رحمت اللہ رعد کے نامی پریس کانپور میں چھپی" (title page checked at zoom). The scan is 225 PDF pages of two-page spreads; Part One is printed pp.1-105.
- Only Part One (Hali's preface and the life, character and opinions of Ghalib) is included. Part Two (printed pp.106-330: anthology of Urdu and Persian verse and prose with Hali's reviews, and the comparisons with Naziri and Zuhuri) is NOT transcribed; it could be a later separate book.

## Chapters
1 About this edition + Introduction (new); 2-17 Hali's Part One divided at natural breaks (Hali has no chapter numbers), each opening with Hali's own heading at that point; 18 Glossary (new, 46 entries).
Hali's footnotes are collected per chapter under "# حواشی" / "# Notes" ("Hali's note:"); interlinear glosses in the print are given as notes too.
Marginal headings: where a heading was faint, the wording of Hali's own table of contents (PDF pp.2-4) was used (e.g. وظیفۂ رامپور، حالات غدر و کتاب دستنبو، قاطع برہان کی تائید، داد سخن، محققانہ نظر، راست گفتاری، اپنے عجز کا اقرار، ہجو نہ لکھنا، اخیر عمر کی حالت، مرض الموت کی حالت، تاریخ وفات، ناؤ نوش کی عادت، اخلاق، فراخ حوصلگی، تقریظ لکھنے کا ڈھنگ، حق پسندی، شاگردوں کی کثرت). Two headings first supplied editorially in transcription (before the Haidar Shikoh vow story, and before the two friends section) were removed, since neither could be read.
All 18 chapters: ur/en block parity checked (same heading flags and line counts per block).
