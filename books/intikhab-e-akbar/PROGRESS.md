Produced by the cloud project thread "University-ready book list" (queue: /mnt/project-files/notes/book-queue.md).

## Source
- Author: Akbar Allahabadi (Sayyid Akbar Husain, 1846-1921). Poems printed in his lifetime (by 1914). Public domain (author died 1921, published before 1931); us_status not needed.
- Urdu text: archive.org `in.ernet.dli.2015.334175` (Digital Library of India), "Kulliyat-e-Akbar, Hissa-e-Awwal", title page: "کلیات اکبر، از تصنیفات عالیجناب خان بہادر سید اکبر حسین صاحب اکبر الہ آبادی، حصہ اول، بار چہارم ۱۹۱۴ء", Standard Press, Allahabad (title page checked). The scan is in reverse page order; each printed page has a full-size and a half-size image.
- Section transcribed: "پیش رو یعنی قطعات", printed pp.157-196, complete except the pieces listed under "Left out" below.
- Other scans checked and not used: dli.ernet.450269 (1912 Indian Press ed., poorer), in.ernet.dli.2015.424972, lisanulasr (1930), KulliyatEAkbarHissaEAwwal (undated, very large).
